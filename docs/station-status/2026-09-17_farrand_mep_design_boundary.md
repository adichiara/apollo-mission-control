# Station research status — Farrand MEP design boundary

Date: 2026-09-17

Status: **visual-simulation technology context strengthened; no station claim promoted**

Primary 1964 Farrand/NASA evidence defines an early MEP as a film-based Earth-orbital visual-image generator within an infinity-image display system. This improves simulator technology context but does not establish a Mission Control console or back-room information path.

No station-level implementation should be added from this evidence. In particular, do not infer controller-visible MEP fields, console pages, telemetry routing, LMS-to-MCC interfaces, or Apollo 13 station use.

Station status remains unchanged pending direct LMS Section 7, `LLR-400-329`, and acceptance/correlation evidence.
