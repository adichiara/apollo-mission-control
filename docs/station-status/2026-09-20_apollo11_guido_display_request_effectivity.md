# Apollo 11 GUIDO — display-request effectivity status

Date: 2026-09-20

## Current status

The Apollo 11 AC Electronics manual is mission-effective authority for MSK-1137 as an LM CRT display format and for its recorded field semantics. Apollo 12 and Apollo 15 manuals independently preserve the same ASPO 45 display-family grouping, which supports format continuity but does not establish Apollo 11 GUIDO ownership or request hardware.

The Apollo 12 Saturn V Flight Manual documents DRK direct-PBI requests and MSK display-request mode as alternate request mechanisms. This remains adjacent-effectivity evidence only.

## Station boundary

Do not implement or depict as historical:

- a specific Apollo 11 GUIDO DRK assignment;
- a GUIDO MSK thumbwheel/key sequence;
- a claim that GUIDO personally requested MSK-1137 during powered descent;
- automatic selection or refresh timing for MSK-1137.

Sourced MSK-1137 field content may be used in a project reconstruction provided the request mechanics are labeled unresolved rather than presented as exact Apollo 11 workflow.

## Evidence status

- **DOCUMENTED:** Apollo 11 MSK-1137 format and field semantics.
- **DOCUMENTED:** ASPO 45 display-family continuity across recovered Apollo 11/12/15 manuals.
- **DOCUMENTED, ADJACENT EFFECTIVITY:** Apollo 12 DRK/MSK request semantics.
- **BLOCKED:** PHO-TN401 direct inspection.
- **UNRESOLVED:** Apollo 11 GUIDO request-device assignment, request sequence, powered-descent format selection, and dynamic-data cadence/latency/freshness.
