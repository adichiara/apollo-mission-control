# Station-status addendum — Mission H-2 TDFCB Revision 4

Date: 2026-09-14  
Parent: `docs/STATION_RESEARCH_STATUS.md`

Historical station maturity grades remain unchanged.

## TELMU

Maturity remains **B**.

Research note 125 adds mission-specific telemetry-configuration provenance: Philco-Ford PHO-TR474 identifies **Mission H-2 TDFCB Revision 4**, delivered 28 January 1970, with special LM Flight Control, high-speed/wideband, index, compare, and PCMGS-related products. The H-2 Rev. 4 TDFCB PCMGS was explicitly checked against PHO-TR155 on 10 February 1970.

This materially improves the evidence chain behind Apollo 13 LM telemetry products, but the actual TDFCB Rev. 4 listings remain unrecovered. Therefore exact TELMU loading, `GC0071V` / `GC0155F` format membership, cadence, CRT/request identity, indicator position, precision, and latency remain unresolved.

## CONTROL

Maturity remains **B**.

The TDFCB finding strengthens the existence of a mission-specific LM telemetry baseline but does not establish that inverter voltage/frequency was presented to CONTROL, nor any exact CONTROL routing or display.

## Reconstruction boundary

Safe statement:

`Mission H-2 TDFCB Rev. 4 telemetry configuration → PCMGS cross-check against PHO-TR155 → MCC display configuration`

Unsafe without recovered source material:

`GC0071V / GC0155F → exact Apollo 13 sample rate → exact TELMU/CONTROL display position`

Next highest-value archive target is **Mission H-2 TDFCB Revision 4**, especially its special LM Flight Control and PCMGS listings.
