# Station status — 2026-09-14 — H-2 PHO-TR155 data-pack process boundary

## TELMU

Maturity remains **B**.

Research note 127 clarifies the provenance chain for Mission H-2 display configuration. PHO-TR515 states that Philco Requirements and Configuration gathered requirements through **data-pack circulation** and prepared **PHO-TR155, Configuration and Control Document**. Preliminary PHO-TR155 working lists then supported downstream display-production work.

This changes the interpretation of the surviving H-2 **data-pack Revision N** reference:

- it is associated with the PHO-TR155 requirements/configuration workflow;
- it is not another name for PHO-TR155 Revision C;
- it does not, by itself, establish TELMU console-09 loading;
- the matching 1970-03-06 dates for Revision N and Revision C do not prove contents identity.

The exact Apollo 13 TELMU inverter presentation remains unresolved: module/indicator location, CRT/display request, MSK/DRK workflow, sample/display cadence, precision, latency, and exact H-2 membership/routing for `GC0071V` / `GC0155F` are still unfrozen.

## Research priority

Highest-value TELMU archival targets are now:

1. Mission H-2 data-pack Revision N or its transmittal/index;
2. a Requirements and Configuration data-pack definition/key for the named pack products;
3. PHO-TR155 Mission H-2 Revision C contents/front matter or TELMU working lists;
4. H-2 TELMU console-09 operational-configuration/loading sheets;
5. Mission H-2 TDFCB Revision 4 special LM Flight Control / PCMGS listings.

No station UI or simulation-model change is warranted from this provenance finding alone.
