# Station status — PC+2 backroom / Staff Support Room boundary

Date: 2026-09-13

Historical station maturity grades remain unchanged. Research note 100 changes scope interpretation, not front-room evidence grades.

## Flight Dynamics stations

**FIDO/RETRO/GUIDO remain B.** Apollo 13 primary evidence confirms that a Flight Dynamics SSR supplied detailed trajectory/guidance analysis and outside technical interfaces to the front-room team. The first playable does not create a separate SSR player or silently grant unsourced support-room calculations to these stations.

## Systems stations

**CONTROL/TELMU remain B.** Apollo 13 support-room architecture included Vehicle Systems SSR functions for detailed systems monitoring, trends, and malfunction analysis. The first playable continues to expose only researched station products/rules/actions. If later play requires a distinct support-generated troubleshooting product, that dependency must be researched explicitly.

## EECOM reference station

**EECOM remains A.** The Apollo 13 Mission Operations Report's explicit praise of EECOM SSR personnel strengthens the project's understanding of front-room/backroom collaboration but does not alter the console/display maturity grade.

## FLIGHT / CAPCOM / INCO

Maturity remains unchanged. Research note 100 does not add support-room-derived products, authority, or voice paths to these stations.

## Scope statement

The supported seven-seat and five-player configurations represent the selected **front-room playable slice**, not the complete historical Apollo 13 Mission Control staffing structure.
