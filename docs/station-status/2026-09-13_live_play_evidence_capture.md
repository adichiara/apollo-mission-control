# Station research status — live-play evidence capture

Date: 2026-09-13

## Status

No controller station receives a new historical capability from research note 095.

The live-play evidence package now requires each material incident to identify the **original station active at the time**, including compact-mode substations. This protects station-specific provenance during physical validation.

For seven-seat play, record incidents against:

- FLIGHT
- CAPCOM
- CONTROL
- TELMU
- GUIDO
- FIDO/RETRO
- INCO

For five-player compact play, player-level labels may be FLIGHT, CAPCOM, LM SYSTEMS, FLIGHT DYNAMICS, and INCO, but incident/action/readiness evidence must continue to identify the original station responsible for the information or action.

## Research consequence

A player report that information was missing does not by itself establish a station research gap. First verify the authoritative station snapshot/audit trail and classify whether the problem is:

- missing sourced mission information;
- incorrect simulation behavior;
- poor station presentation;
- browser/network behavior;
- player instruction; or
- legitimate controller uncertainty.

Only the first category automatically enters historical source review.

See research note 095, `docs/testing/PC2_LIVE_PLAYTEST_PROTOCOL.md`, and `docs/testing/PC2_LIVE_PLAYTEST_REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`.
