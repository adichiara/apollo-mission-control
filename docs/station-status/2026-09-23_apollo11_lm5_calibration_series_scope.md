# Station research status — LM-5 operational-calibration series scope

Date: 2026-09-23

## CONTROL

**Status: PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED; interpretation tightened.**

The 12 Jun 1969 **Operational Calibration Curves, Vol. II — Calibration Curves for LM-5** remains documented by the NASA-MSC records transmittal at **FRC accession 72-A-1116 / RG 255 / agency box 71** and remains blocked on document recovery.

New primary-series evidence from NASA/NTRS record **19720018200 / NASA-TM-X-68322** describes the later Apollo 16 operational-calibration family as calibration curves for **PCM downlink and onboard display**, with measurement identification, multi-mode calibration summaries, PCM bilevel-event listings, and instrumentation/meter-link calibration curves. This is strong evidence about the series' function, but not about LM-5-specific channels or values.

### Consequence

Do not describe the unrecovered LM-5 volume as a likely physical DPS/FTP force-calibration source. Recover it to establish actual LM-5 telemetry/display measurement conversions and channel coverage. For the unresolved FTP-force question, prioritize **Mayer 69-FMZ2-149** and the two 8 Jul 1969 throttle-down dispersion studies, then the LM-5 DPS final-flight-evaluation/engine-performance records.

Purdue also catalogs an **Apollo 11/LM-5 Post Mission Report, 5 Aug 1969**. It is a secondary recovery lead only; no content claim is made from the catalog title.
