# Station research status — LM-5 operational-calibration series scope

Date: 2026-09-23

## CONTROL

**Status: PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED; direct recovery path narrowed.**

The 12 Jun 1969 **Operational Calibration Curves, Vol. II — Calibration Curves for LM-5** remains documented by the NASA-MSC records transmittal at **FRC accession 72-A-1116 / RG 255 / agency box 71** and remains blocked on document recovery.

Primary-series evidence from NASA/NTRS record **19720018200 / NASA-TM-X-68322** describes the later Apollo operational-calibration family as calibration curves for **PCM downlink and onboard display**, with measurement identification, multi-mode calibration summaries, PCM bilevel-event listings, and instrumentation/meter-link calibration curves. This is strong evidence about the series' function, but not about LM-5-specific channels or values.

### Direct FTP target

The official Purdue University Archives catalog now locates the 5 Jun 1969 FTP study at **Neil A. Armstrong papers, MSA 5, Series 1, Box 58, Folder 4, Item 10**; hierarchical identifier **MSA 5, Series 1, Sub-Series 5, Sub-Group 4, File 6, Item 16**. Its title/date match the independent NASA/MSC citation to John P. Mayer memorandum **69-FMZ2-149**.

The companion 8 Jul studies are in **MSA 5, Box 54, Folder 5, Items 14–15**. Item 15 is explicitly cataloged as **MSA 5, Series 1, Sub-Series 5, Sub-Group 4, File 2, Item 21**. These locations support a precise archival reproduction request; they do not establish any numerical result.

### Consequence

Do not describe the unrecovered LM-5 Operational Calibration Curves as a likely physical DPS/FTP force-calibration source. For the unresolved FTP-force question, first recover **Box 58/Folder 4/Item 10**, then **Box 54/Folder 5/Items 14–15**, then pursue LM-5 DPS final-flight-evaluation/engine-performance records. Recover the LM-5 calibration volume separately for actual telemetry/display measurement conversions and channel coverage.

Purdue also catalogs an **Apollo 11/LM-5 Post Mission Report, 5 Aug 1969**. It remains a secondary recovery lead only; no content claim is made from the catalog title.
