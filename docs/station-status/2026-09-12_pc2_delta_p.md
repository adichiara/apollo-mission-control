# Station Status Addendum — PC+2 fuel/oxidizer differential pressure

Date: 2026-09-12  
Parent: `docs/STATION_RESEARCH_STATUS.md`

## CONTROL

Maturity remains **B**.

Research note `058_pc2_fuel_oxidizer_delta_p_observation_path.md` advances one PC+2 CONTROL rule from deferred to bounded executable status.

Mission-specific primary sources establish that:

- fuel/oxidizer ΔP greater than **25 psi** was a PC+2 shutdown criterion;
- it was specifically a **ground callout** to the crew;
- it was a distinct criterion from the 150-psi ground engine-inlet-pressure rule and the 85-psi ground chamber-pressure rule.

The executable model now permits an optional CONTROL product:

- `dps.fuel_oxidizer_delta_p_psi`

This is intentionally modeled as a **ground-derived propulsion-monitoring product**, not as a direct subtraction of LM source transducers.

Still unresolved:

- exact Apollo 13 ground computation/sign convention;
- whether `GQ3611P` and `GQ4111P` were the direct inputs to the displayed/monitored ΔP quantity;
- filtering/scaling/limit logic;
- exact CONTROL CRT/MSK field and update cadence;
- the separate singular 150-psi inlet-pressure selection/aggregation semantics.

Therefore CONTROL remains maturity B. The new implementation improves rule-path fidelity without resolving the larger display/ground-processing reconstruction gap.
