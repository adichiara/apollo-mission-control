# Apollo 11 R-567 LUMINARY 1A coordination status

Date: 2026-09-18

This coordination note keeps the D-024 portfolio, roadmap, progress, station research, and source catalog aligned after recovery of the June 1969 LUMINARY 1A `R-567` Section 2 control sheet and the January 1969 SCB-28 LUMINARY 1A configuration-control memo.

## Portfolio status

The Apollo 11 powered-descent exception remains **OPEN**, but its source-recovery path is narrower. `69-FS-3` is no longer the only controlled route: Apollo 11-effective `R-567` revisions and contemporaneous LUMINARY configuration-control records are parallel primary-source families.

The current bounded unresolved item is the **LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099 Section 5 Guidance Equations effectivity/change state**, with priority on landing-program/P63-P66 material associated with PCR-670 and final implementation evidence for PCR-700.

## New controlled boundary

MIT/IL Apollo Project Memo 7-69 (23 January 1969) identifies LUMINARY 1A as the Mission G program and records PCR-700 and PCR-670 among approved landing changes. It describes intended P66 behavior for PCR-700 and assigns Section 5 GSOP action for PCR-670 to Allan Klumpp. It also records PCR-704, -705, -706, and -723 as disapproved in that board state.

The June 1969 Section 2 Rev. 4 control sheet independently places PCR-670 and PCR-700 in a LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099 document state. Together these sources improve the change-control crosswalk, but they do not substitute for the missing Section 5 technical pages or final program implementation evidence.

## Consistency rule

Configuration-control approval, action assignments, and change titles do not by themselves prove final flight implementation. No equation, constant, cadence, DSKY behavior, radar weighting rule, player-visible product, or runtime behavior may be promoted until final Rev. 099 implementation/effectivity is demonstrated.

Later LUMINARY 1B/1C R-567 material remains comparison evidence unless a controlled change chain or independent Apollo 11-period source establishes applicability.
