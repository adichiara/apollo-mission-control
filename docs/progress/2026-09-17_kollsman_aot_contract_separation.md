# Progress — Kollsman AOT contract separation

Date: 2026-09-17

Research note 231 resolves one ambiguity introduced by the KSC LMS AOT provenance work.

Primary NASA NTRS records for Kollsman's 1970 *Apollo Optical Subsystem and LM Alignment Optical Telescope* final-report set identify the governing funding contract as **NAS9-497**; the original Volume I foreword likewise names NASA ACED contract `NAS 9-497`. This is distinct from `NAS9-8634`, the contract identified in the RG 255 E.155B1 LMS project-file series description.

Repository consequence:

- do not use the KSC LMS AOT object provenance as evidence that `NAS9-8634` procured the AOT;
- maintain `NAS9-497` AOT/optical-subsystem and `NAS9-8634` LMS archival work as separate contract trails unless a primary source explicitly connects them;
- continue E.155B/E.155B1 retrieval for MEP/EVDE and Apollo 13-era LMS configuration;
- historical station maturity remains unchanged because no Mission Control routing or simulator-output mapping was recovered.

Primary records: NTRS `19700015843`, `19700015844`, `19700015845`.
