# Progress — Apollo 11 R-567 Luminary 1A control

Date: 2026-09-18

The Apollo 11 powered-descent source search recovered a directly readable primary GSOP control sheet rather than another secondary reconstruction.

## Finding

`R-567`, Section 2, **Data Links (Revision 4)** is dated June 1969 and explicitly names **LUMINARY 1A (Rev. 099)**. Its revision index identifies several Apollo 11-era approved changes relevant to the current research boundary: landing-program simplification (PCR-670), P66 rate-of-descent performance (PCR-700), new propulsion-system constants (PCN-765), and a Section 2 documentation update (PCR-824).

The Luminary 99/1 program listing states that LUMINARY implementation details are specified in `R-567, as amended`, independently tying the flown-program family to the GSOP.

## What changed

- Added R-567 LUMINARY 1A revision/change pages as a parallel primary retrieval path alongside `69-FS-3`.
- Narrowed the next target to Apollo 11-effective **Section 5 Guidance Equations** control/change pages and the specific powered-descent/radar/update pages they govern.
- Preserved the effectivity boundary: later LUMINARY 1B/1C GSOP material remains comparative only unless a controlled change comparison supports use.

## What did not change

No equation, constant, landing-radar model, P66 behavior, station maturity, or executable behavior was promoted from the revision-index titles alone.
