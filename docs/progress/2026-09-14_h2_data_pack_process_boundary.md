# Progress Log — 2026-09-14 — H-2 data-pack process boundary

## Research completed

Primary-source follow-on to research note 126 examined how Apollo-era PHO-TR155 data packs fit the Mission Control display-configuration workflow.

Philco PHO-TR515 states that the Requirements and Configuration Section gathered information through **data-pack circulation** and prepared **PHO-TR155, Configuration and Control Document**, with computer listings. The same manual shows preliminary PHO-TR155 working lists feeding downstream display-production work such as module overlays.

Combined with PHO-TR460 and PHO-TR474, this establishes the process boundary:

`display/user requirement → data-pack circulation → Requirements & Configuration → PHO-TR155 configuration/control output → working lists/display implementation`

The Mission H-2 **data-pack Revision N** reference is therefore not a synonym for PHO-TR155 Revision C and is not, by itself, evidence of a TELMU console-loading package. Its 1970-03-06 date matches Revision C, but the reviewed evidence does not establish one-to-one contents identity.
