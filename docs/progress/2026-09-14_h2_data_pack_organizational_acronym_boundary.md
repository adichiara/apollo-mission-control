# Progress — H-2 data-pack organizational-acronym boundary

Date: 2026-09-14

Continued the Mission H-2 PHO-TR155 archival track after research note 127.

Primary-source organization records now establish that `FCOB` and `LSB` were contemporary Apollo flight-control acronyms for **Flight Control Operations Branch** and **LM Systems Branch**. The same MSC organization listing also identifies CSM Systems, Flight Dynamics, Mission Control Requirements, and Experiments Systems branches.

This narrows archival search terms but does **not** establish that identically shaped PHO-TR460 data-pack labels were branch-owned packs or prove expansions for `CSB`, `FDB`, `ESB`, `SLV`, `FSB`, `MOD`, `PCB`, or `OSB`. Research note 128 records that boundary explicitly.

No simulation/UI behavior changes are justified. Exact H-2 TELMU console-09 loading, `GC0071V` / `GC0155F` routing, cadence, precision, latency, and Revision-N contents remain unresolved.

Next targets remain the actual Mission H-2 data-pack Revision N/transmittal, a PHO-TR155 pack key/distribution record, PHO-TR155 Revision C working lists, and H-2 TDFCB Revision 4 LM Flight Control/PCMGS material.
