# Progress — SC-012 AMS source-control boundary

Date: 2026-09-18

Research continuation checked the newly public **Apollo Mission Simulator Instructor Handbook, Spacecraft 012** family against primary bibliographic/archival control before using it as an LMS analogue.

Resolved:

- NASA/KSC `GP-642` identifies Volume II as North American Aviation `NAA-SID-65-974-2`, **Instructor Workbook**, dated **15 April 1966**.
- Virginia Tech identifies the companion Volume I holding as **AS-204A / Description and Utilization**, also dated 15 April 1966.
- The pair is therefore controlled as an early **SC-012 / AS-204A Command Module AMS** source family, not an Apollo 13 LM LMS configuration source.

This prevents a newly accessible and unusually detailed simulator manual from being silently back-projected into the 1970 LMS. It may be mined later for explicitly labeled cross-simulator architecture comparisons, but LMS-specific claims still require LMS evidence.

No station maturity, causal constants, scenario effectivity, or executable behavior changed.
