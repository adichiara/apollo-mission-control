# Progress — Apollo 11 descent alarm radio timing

Date: 2026-09-24
Research note: 518

The primary GOSS NET 1 transcript was inspected at the Mission Report alarm windows rather than used only as a source-scope record.

Mission-G radio anchors recovered:

- 102:38:30/32 — crew explicitly identifies 1202;
- 102:38:48 — Armstrong requests a reading on the 1202 alarm;
- 102:38:53 — CAPCOM gives the GO on that alarm;
- 102:42:19 — CAPCOM acknowledges the program alarm;
- 102:42:24 — Armstrong identifies 1201;
- 102:42:25 — CAPCOM gives the GO / same-type disposition;
- 102:42:58 — CAPCOM acknowledges a subsequent 1202.

Cross-checking against the Mission Report demonstrates that computer/event timestamps and spoken timestamps are distinct. This closes the radio-side timing sufficiently for the next internal-audio comparison, but does not establish the Garman/Bales internal timing or circuit.
