# Kollsman / KSC LMS AOT provenance

Date: 2026-09-17

Research note 230 adds an independent authoritative object-provenance result to the unresolved Kollsman LMS contract workstream.

The Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum records object `A19751446001` as a functioning **Alignment Optical Telescope** manufactured by **Kollsman Instrument Company**, designed by MIT Instrumentation Laboratory, and originally installed in the **Lunar Module Simulator used for astronaut mission training at Kennedy Space Center**. The LMS including the telescope was transferred to the Smithsonian in 1974.

This directly establishes a specific Kollsman-manufactured component in the KSC LMS. It does **not** establish that the AOT was procured under `NAS9-8634`, that the contract covered MEP/EVDE, that Kollsman replaced Farrand, or that the object record establishes Apollo 13 configuration.
