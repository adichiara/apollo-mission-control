# Progress — Apollo 11 descent voice evidence scope

Date: 2026-09-24
Research note: 517

The next unresolved voice item was checked against NASA's primary transcript catalog. NTRS document 20160014392 identifies the Apollo 11 technical transcript specifically as **GOSS NET 1 / air-to-ground voice**. NASA History's transcript collection independently catalogs `AS11_TEC.PDF` as the Apollo 11 Technical Air-to-Ground Voice Transcription.

This narrows the evidence model. `AS11_TEC` can support Eagle/CAPCOM radio wording and sequence, but it cannot support the internal Garman → Bales → Flight exchange, named conference loop, or internal overlap. Those claims still require direct GUIDO L/R and/or `792-AAI` inspection.

The Mission Report's five alarm times remain event-search anchors. The project will not back-infer unsupported internal dialogue from a later CAPCOM transmission.
