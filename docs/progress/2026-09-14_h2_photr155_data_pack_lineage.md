# Progress — Mission H-2 PHO-TR155 data-pack lineage

Date: 2026-09-14

## Research target

Continue from research note 125 and clarify whether the surviving reference to **Mission H-2 data-pack Revision N** can safely be treated as a TELMU console-loading artifact.

## Primary-source result

Philco-Ford **PHO-TR460** shows that earlier Mission H-2 PHO-TR155 configuration work used multiple individually named data-pack products, including `FDB`, `CSB`, `FCOB`, `LSB`, `SLV`, `FSB`, `ESB`, `MOD`, `PCB`, and `OSB` revisions, and also records a preliminary H-2 card deck/listing issued to IBM.

Philco-Ford **PHO-TR474** later reports H-2 **data-pack Revision N** on 6 March 1970, the same date as PHO-TR155 Revision C, but does not identify Revision N as TELMU-specific or define the earlier pack abbreviations in the reviewed passage.

## Interpretation

The archival target needs to be more precise. “Revision N” is a valid mission-specific identifier, but it is not evidence by itself that the missing artifact is a TELMU console-09 loading sheet.

The safest next targets are:

- Mission H-2 TDFCB Revision 4 and its special LM Flight Control / PCMGS listings;
- PHO-TR155 Mission H-2 Revision C;
- a PHO-TR155 data-pack key/index or contents list that maps the pack abbreviations and Revision N scope;
- Revision N itself;
- direct H-2 TELMU operational-configuration/loading material.

No generic acronym expansion is promoted into the historical record.
