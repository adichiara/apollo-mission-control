# Progress — RG 255 E.155B provenance boundary

Date: 2026-09-17

Research note 300 re-read the primary RG 255 inventory at series-description level and corrected the retrieval model for E.155B.

E.155B is **not** a dedicated LMS visual-system series. It is the 20-foot *Reference Files on the Lunar Landing Training Vehicle* series, whose inventory description says it also contains some material relating to the LMS Mission Effects Projector and External Visual Display Equipment. E.155B1 is separately and explicitly the dedicated 1968–1970 *Project Files on the Lunar Module Simulator*.

Repository consequence: retain E.155B as a direct primary-source lead, but request/search it narrowly for MEP/EVDE rather than treating the whole LLTV/LLRV archival neighborhood as LMS evidence. E.155B1 / `72A794` remains the first dedicated LMS retrieval target.

No technical relationship between LLTV/LLRV and LMS MEP/EVDE, no `NAS9-8634` subsystem scope, no Mission Control routing, and no Apollo 13 effectivity was inferred.

Updated: roadmap addendum, station-status boundary, MEP source catalog, and research note 300.
