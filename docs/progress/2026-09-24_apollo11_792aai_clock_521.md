# Progress — Apollo 11 `792-AAI` clock reconciliation

Date: 2026-09-24
Research note: 521

Resolved the semantic clock used by the cataloged `792-AAI` lunar-descent range. DVIDS' U.S. Government catalog entry identifies the Flight Director-loop artifact as `Lunar Descent 1955-2025`; NASA independently records lunar landing at 20:17:43 GMT / GET 102:45:43 and range zero at 13:32:00 GMT on 16 July. The catalog range is consequently a GMT/time-of-day window, approximately GET 102:23–102:53.

All five previously established 1201/1202 event anchors fall inside that interval. This removes the need to guess what the catalog numbers mean, but does not justify assuming a digitized file starts sample-exactly at 19:55:00.
