# Progress — LMS handbook archival identity

Date: 2026-09-17

## Completed

Research notes 239–242 continued the unresolved LMS machine-allocation and visual-system thread with primary-source-first retrieval.

The NASA Manned Spacecraft Center's July 1968 *Apollo Engineering and Technology Index* supplies exact MSC accession identifiers for the Grumman `LMA-790-2-LMS` handbook family under `NAS9-1100`: `67-14186` (Volume I, Simulator Description), `67-14187` and `67-14188` (Volume II operating sections), and `67-16127` (Volume II, Section 7, Simulator Output Tables).

Research note 240 located a public digitized candidate with the matching Volume I title in the Virtual AGC collection. The surfaced discovery metadata does not expose enough scan-level identity to treat it as recovered `67-14186`.

Research note 241 added independent NASA/KSC GP-642 confirmation mapping the exact Volume I title to GAEC `LMA 790-2-LMS`, dated Apr. 1, 1967. The date discrepancy remains preserved: the MSC main index and GP-642 support 01-04-67, while the MSC supplement gives 15-05-67.

Research note 242 adds a second exact primary retrieval key from the NASA/MSC corporate-author index: Grumman `LLR-400-329`, **Contract NAS9-1100, LMS Mission Effects Projector Program Review**, dated 06-07-67, MSC accession `67-15490`. This establishes the existence and archival identity of a contemporaneous Grumman MEP program review, but not its technical contents or relationship to later Kollsman `NAS9-8634` work.

No workload, cadence, MEP architecture, EVDE relationship, Mission Control routing, acceptance tolerance, or Apollo 13 configuration claim was promoted.
