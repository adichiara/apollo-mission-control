# Progress — LMS handbook archival identity

Date: 2026-09-17

## Completed

Research notes 239–240 continued the unresolved LMS machine-allocation thread with primary-source-first retrieval.

The NASA Manned Spacecraft Center's July 1968 *Apollo Engineering and Technology Index* supplies exact MSC accession identifiers for the Grumman `LMA-790-2-LMS` handbook family under `NAS9-1100`: `67-14186` (Volume I, Simulator Description), `67-14187` and `67-14188` (Volume II operating sections), and `67-16127` (Volume II, Section 7, Simulator Output Tables).

Research note 240 located a public digitized candidate with the matching Volume I title in the Virtual AGC collection. The collection describes it as a handbook for the LEM Mission Simulator at the Cape and says it contains switch/display and subsystem-simulation detail. The surfaced discovery metadata does not expose the Grumman report number, MSC accession, contract, date/revision, or title page, so it has **not** yet been treated as the recovered `67-14186` copy.

A bibliographic date discrepancy was also preserved rather than normalized: the main MSC index gives 01-04-67 for `67-14186`/`67-14187`, its supplement gives 15-05-67, and NASA KSC bibliography GP-642 lists the handbook as Apr. 1, 1967.

No workload, cadence, common-memory-map, Mission Control routing, or Apollo 13 configuration claim was promoted.
