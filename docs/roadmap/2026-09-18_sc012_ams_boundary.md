# Roadmap addendum — SC-012 AMS handbook boundary

Date: 2026-09-18  
Parent: `docs/ROADMAP.md`

## Newly resolved

A primary NASA/NTRS bibliography identifies **Apollo Mission Simulator Instructor Handbook (Spacecraft 012 Configuration), Volume II — Instructor Workbook**, North American Aviation, **15 April 1966**, report `NAA-SID-65-974-2` (NASA bibliography item 208). The Virginia Tech Avitabile finding aid independently identifies the companion Volume I holding as **AS-204A / Volume 1 — Description and Utilization**, also dated 15 April 1966.

This fixes an important retrieval boundary: the newly public SC-012 AMS handbook pair is an **early Block I Command Module / AS-204A simulator source family**, not an Apollo 13 LM LMS configuration source.

## Roadmap consequence

The SC-012 manuals remain useful as adjacent Apollo simulator evidence for architecture, instructor-workbook structure, malfunction/output abstractions, and simulator-document organization. They must not be used to establish Apollo 13 LMS effectivity, LM malfunction availability, LMS scripting syntax, LM subsystem equations, or 1970 instructor procedures without independent LMS-period corroboration.

Immediate LMS priority therefore remains unchanged:

1. page-verify the 1967 `LMA-790-2-LMS` Volume I candidate;
2. retrieve Volume II Section 2 **Malfunction Data**;
3. continue controlled Apollo 13 `SKB32100076-386` symptom/procedure extraction;
4. retrieve Section 7 **Simulator Output Tables** and Section 6 **Scripting Data Sheets**.

No executable model, station maturity, or historical scenario claim changes from this boundary check.
