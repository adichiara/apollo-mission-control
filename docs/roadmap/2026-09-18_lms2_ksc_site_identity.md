# Roadmap addendum — LMS-2 KSC site identity

Date: 2026-09-18  
Parent: `docs/ROADMAP.md`

## Newly resolved

Research note 305 recovers a contemporaneous NASA Apollo 11 crew-training planning guide whose 19 May 1969 schedule explicitly pairs `LOI Sim-KSC` with `LMS #2`. The project may therefore treat **LMS-2 = KSC** as primary-supported for this operational context.

This strengthens research note 304's interpretation of the 1973 KSC `LMS-2 system` disposition record without changing its survivor/subset boundary.

## Active priority

1. Recover `LMA-790-2-LMS` Volume I and Volume II/Section 7 for machine/program/output ownership.
2. Search KSC program-loading, maintenance, configuration, property, or disposition records for DDP-224 serial/function mapping.
3. Retrieve RG 255 E.155B1 acceptance/configuration records and establish date/site effectivity.
4. Crosswalk recovered generic/deployed architecture to Apollo 13 only after machine ownership is established.

Do not infer LMS-2 machine count, workload allocation, or Apollo 13 configuration from the site identity alone.
