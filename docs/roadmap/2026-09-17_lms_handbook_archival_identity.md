# Roadmap update — LMS handbook archival identity

Date: 2026-09-17

## Current state

Research notes 235, 236, 238, 239, 240, and 241 now constrain both the LMS compute-topology evidence and the next primary retrieval path.

The mature LMS is supported as a three-machine complex with one machine dedicated to onboard-guidance-computer simulation. The original two machine workloads remain unknown. NASA MSC bibliographic control provides exact accession keys for the 1967 Grumman LMS instructor handbook. NASA/KSC GP-642 independently maps the exact Volume I title to GAEC `LMA 790-2-LMS`, dated Apr. 1, 1967. A public digitized Volume I title-match candidate is known, but its identity as the particular `67-14186` accession copy is not yet proven from the scan itself.

## Priority retrieval sequence

1. **Digitized Volume I identity gate** — inspect the candidate scan's title/revision pages. The report-number/title crosswalk is now independently established by NASA GP-642; require the scan itself to establish `LMA-790-2-LMS`, date/revision, and site applicability before treating it as the accession copy.
2. **`67-14186` body** — once identity is established, search Volume I for computer-complex description, DDP-224 roles, interfaces, program allocation, digital conversion, guidance-computer simulation, and common-memory architecture.
3. **`67-16127`** — Volume II, Section 7, Simulator Output Tables. Determine whether outputs are mapped to machine/program ownership or only to simulator interfaces.
4. **`67-14187` / `67-14188`** — Volume II operating sections if machine/program allocation remains unresolved.
5. **RG 255 E.155B1 / program-loading records** — pursue date/site effectivity and later configuration changes, including the Kollsman `NAS9-8634` period.
6. **Apollo 13-period configuration records** — only after generic/deployed architecture is bounded.

## Metadata caution

Do not silently reconcile the `67-14186`/`67-14187` date discrepancy. Two primary NASA controls now support 01-04-67: the MSC main index and KSC GP-642. The MSC supplement gives 15-05-67. Treat the latter as unresolved until revision/catalog evidence explains it.
