# Roadmap update — LMS handbook archival identity

Date: 2026-09-17

## Current state

Research notes 235, 236, 238, 239, and 240 now constrain both the LMS compute-topology evidence and the next primary retrieval path.

The mature LMS is supported as a three-machine complex with one machine dedicated to onboard-guidance-computer simulation. The original two machine workloads remain unknown. NASA MSC bibliographic control provides exact accession keys for the 1967 Grumman LMS instructor handbook, and a public digitized Volume I title-match candidate has now been located. Its identity with accession `67-14186` is not yet proven.

## Priority retrieval sequence

1. **Digitized Volume I identity gate** — inspect the candidate scan's title/revision pages. Require sufficient evidence to connect it to `LMA-790-2-LMS` / Grumman / `NAS9-1100` / `67-14186`, or explicitly record why accession identity cannot be established.
2. **`67-14186` body** — once identity is established, search Volume I for computer-complex description, DDP-224 roles, interfaces, program allocation, digital conversion, guidance-computer simulation, and common-memory architecture.
3. **`67-16127`** — Volume II, Section 7, Simulator Output Tables. Determine whether outputs are mapped to machine/program ownership or only to simulator interfaces.
4. **`67-14187` / `67-14188`** — Volume II operating sections if machine/program allocation remains unresolved.
5. **RG 255 E.155B1 / program-loading records** — pursue date/site effectivity and later configuration changes, including the Kollsman `NAS9-8634` period.
6. **Apollo 13-period configuration records** — only after generic/deployed architecture is bounded.

## Metadata caution

Do not silently reconcile the `67-14186`/`67-14187` date discrepancy: the main MSC index gives 01-04-67, the supplement gives 15-05-67, and NASA KSC bibliography GP-642 lists the handbook as Apr. 1, 1967. Treat revision/date identity as part of the scan-verification step.

## Gate

Do not assign the original two machines to causal domains or station products from architecture count alone. Close that allocation only with configuration-controlled evidence or demonstrate that the allocation is irrelevant to player-visible products under D-022. A matching web title alone is not sufficient provenance for implementation claims.
