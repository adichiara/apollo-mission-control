# Roadmap addendum — LMS DDP-224 procurement-complex boundary

Date: 2026-09-18  
Parent: `docs/ROADMAP.md`

## Newly bounded

NASA SP-4009 identifies contemporaneous NASA News Release `66-254` (21 Sep. 1966) as the source for the $4.2 million Honeywell Apollo simulator computer award. The procurement is described as **six separate computer complexes** supporting CM/LM simulators at MSC and Cape Kennedy, with planned delivery/installation/checkout by end-March 1967.

This is a complex-level procurement count, not a DDP-224 processor count. It must not be used to reconcile later two/three/four-machine statements.

1. Recover original NASA News Release `66-254` and Honeywell contract/acceptance material defining the six complexes.
2. Recover `LMA-790-2-LMS` Volume I and Section 7 for machine/program/output ownership.
3. Search program-loading, maintenance, configuration, property, and disposition records for DDP-224 serial/function mapping.
4. Retrieve RG 255 E.155B1 acceptance/configuration records and establish date/site effectivity.

Preserve the current 21 Jul. versus 21 Sep. 1966 contract-date discrepancy until the underlying primary contracting record is inspected.
