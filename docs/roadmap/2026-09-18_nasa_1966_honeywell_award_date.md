# Roadmap addendum — NASA 1966 Honeywell award-date corroboration

Date: 2026-09-18  
Parent: `docs/ROADMAP.md`

## Newly bounded

Research note 308 adds a second independent NASA chronology that places the Honeywell simulator-computer award on **21 September 1966** and explicitly cites NASA Release `66-254`. The September date is therefore strongly corroborated within NASA's published record, while Tomayko's later 21 July date remains a discrepancy until the primary release or contracting record is inspected.

The procurement unit remains **six computer complexes**, not six processors.
