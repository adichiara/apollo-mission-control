## Resolved in this pass

Research note 111 closes the first-playable identity gap left by notes 059–060:

- the LM Operations Handbook specifies **inverter 1** as the operating inverter during DPS/APS burns;
- Apollo 13's PC+2 procedure prepared **inverter 2** before the maneuver;
- the contemporaneous PC+2 shutdown rule required trying the other inverter before a continuing inverter light became a shutdown criterion.

For the current PC+2 slice, use the source-bounded interpretation:

`inverter 1 operating → caution/light → attempt transfer to inverter 2 → continuing light → shutdown criterion`

This supersedes earlier roadmap wording that listed the **alternate-inverter identity** itself as unresolved.
