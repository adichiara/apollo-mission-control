# Roadmap addendum — RG 255 LMS visual-system retrieval

Date: 2026-09-17

## Newly resolved

The primary RG 255 inventory supplies a direct archival home for MEP/EVDE material (E.155B) and a later 1968–1970 LMS project-file series (E.155B1) with explicit Kollsman / `NAS9-8634` retrieval keys.

Primary NASA/Kollsman final-report records now establish a separate contract identity for the Apollo Optical Subsystem and LM Alignment Optical Telescope program: **NAS9-497**. The KSC LMS AOT provenance therefore must not be used by itself to assign that hardware to `NAS9-8634`.

## Still unresolved

The relationship among the 1967 Farrand MEP, the E.155B MEP/EVDE records, Kollsman's `NAS9-8634` work, and the Apollo 13-era LMS configuration is not established. Whether `NAS9-8634` included any AOT support, modification, interfacing, or other optical work also remains unresolved.

## Next sequence

1. Retrieve E.155B MEP/EVDE records using the series name, old accessions, and inventory locations.
2. Retrieve E.155B1 records keyed to `NAS9-8634`, Kollsman, MEP, EVDE, acceptance, statement of work, and surface models; treat AOT as a cross-reference rather than assumed scope.
3. Keep `NAS9-497` AOT/optical-subsystem and `NAS9-8634` LMS records as separate contract trails unless explicit primary text connects them.
4. Build a dated vendor/configuration matrix only from explicit document statements.
5. Crosswalk recovered records against `LLR-400-329` / `*67-15490` and Section 7 / `*67-16127`.
6. Do not infer Mission Control routing or Apollo 13 effectivity until directly supported.
