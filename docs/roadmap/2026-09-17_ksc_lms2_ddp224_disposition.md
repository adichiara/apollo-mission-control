# Roadmap addendum — KSC LMS-2 DDP-224 disposition

Date: 2026-09-17  
Parent: `docs/ROADMAP.md`

## Newly resolved

Primary KSC evidence now establishes a post-Apollo disposition point: two DDP-224 systems explicitly described as having been used in the Apollo-program **LMS-2 system** were selected for KSC SUMC breadboard reuse in 1973.

## Boundary retained

This does not replace the mature three-machine LMS architecture in NASA TN D-7112 and does not identify which Apollo-era machines/functions survived. Treat 1973 “two systems” as a retained subset unless a complete disposition/configuration record proves otherwise.

## Active priority

1. Recover `LMA-790-2-LMS` Volume I and Volume II/Section 7 for machine/program/output ownership.
2. Search program-loading, maintenance, configuration, property, or disposition records for DDP-224 serial/function mapping.
3. Retrieve RG 255 E.155B1 acceptance/configuration records and establish date/site effectivity.
4. Only after that crosswalk the architecture to Apollo 13 training dates.

Do not close the Houston four-machine discrepancy by inference.
