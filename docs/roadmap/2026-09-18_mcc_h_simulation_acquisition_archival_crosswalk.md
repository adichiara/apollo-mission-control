## Newly bounded

Wichita State Box 40 FF 91 is now crosswalked to Christopher C. Kraft, Jr.'s 13 January 1966 memorandum *Recommended Change to the Mission Control Center-Houston (MCC-H) Apollo Simulation Computer Acquisition Plan*, archived in JSC History Collection Box 066-44. NASA/JSC HAER documentation reports that the proposal sought five IBM 360 computers.

This is an MCC-H simulation-computing acquisition thread, not evidence for the Honeywell DDP-224 LMS/CMS procurement. Do not merge the five-IBM and six-Honeywell-complex counts.
