## Guardrail

The archival **Changed** label is evidence of a changed holding, not a recovered revision number or technical delta. Bibliographic identity and finding-aid titles do not authorize Apollo 13 processor assignments, software revisions, loading procedures, telemetry routing, scripting semantics, malfunction behavior, numerical cadence, acceptance tolerances, station-product changes, or executable constants.

The 16 March Apollo 13 crew malfunction procedures can now be treated as a controlled mission-state source for player-visible symptoms and operational response, but cannot establish LMS instructor controls, scripting syntax, internal failure representation, or injectability. The later 1 April Change A state must remain separate until its delta is recovered.

The existing causal-engine measurement/output layer and Causal Model Lab already satisfy the architectural consequence of recovered output-dictionary evidence. Do not add duplicate runtime abstractions merely because another historical source supports the same separation.
