## Newly useful change-control identifiers

From the June 1969 Section 2 Rev. 4 control sheet:

- PCR-670 — *Simplification of Landing Programs*
- PCR-700 — *Improve the Rate of Descent Mode (P66) Performance*
- PCN-765 — *New Propulsion System Constants*
- PCR-824 — Section 2 documentation/detail update

These identifiers are retrieval/crosswalk keys. They do not establish the detailed algorithmic change without the affected technical pages or change documentation.

## Retrieval priority

1. `R-567` Section 5 **Guidance Equations** control/revision pages effective for LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099.
2. Affected landing-radar/update, estimator/filter, P63–P66, and propulsion-constant pages.
3. `69-FS-3`, retained as the preferred complete programmed-guidance-equation source when recoverable.
4. Controlled comparison with later LUMINARY revisions only after effectivity/change boundaries are explicit.
