# Research note 323 — LMS Volume II Sections 5 and 6 accession control

Date: 2026-09-18

## Question

What does the primary NASA/MSC bibliographic record establish about `LMA-790-2-LMS`, Volume II, Sections 5 and 6, and how should they be handled relative to the other handbook issues?

## Primary-source result

The NASA/MSC *Corporate Author Index* separately catalogs:

- report: `LMA-790-2-LMS`;
- issue date: **1 April 1967**;
- MSC accession: **67-14188**;
- contract: `NAS9-1100`;
- title/content: *Lunar Module Mission Simulator Instructors Handbook, Volume 2 — Simulator Operation Sections 5 and 6*.

Primary index scan: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARASWoverflow/CorporateIndex.pdf

The same primary index places `67-14188` adjacent to `67-14186` (Volume I — Simulator Description) and `67-14187` (Volume II Sections 1 and 4). Unlike `67-14186` and `67-14187`, the later Corporate Author Index Supplement evidence currently recovered in this project does **not** provide a conflicting 15 May entry for `67-14188`.

## Interpretation boundary

This closes a bibliographic-control point only. It establishes that Sections 5 and 6 were grouped under their own accession and that the recovered NASA/MSC record assigns them a 1 April 1967 issue date.

It does **not** establish:

- the titles or technical subject matter of Sections 5 or 6 individually;
- that their content was unchanged after 1 April 1967;
- that their effectivity matches Sections 1-4 or separately issued Section 7;
- Apollo 13 configuration or site-specific applicability;
- processor/program ownership, common-memory mapping, output routing, numerical cadence, or executable constants.

Do not infer a 15 May issue date for `67-14188` merely because adjacent `67-14186` and `67-14187` have conflicting April/May NASA/MSC records. Conversely, absence of a recovered conflicting supplement entry is not proof that no later issue or revision existed.

## Repository consequence

- Preserve `67-14188` / **1 April 1967** as the exact retrieval key for Volume II Sections 5 and 6.
- Keep Sections 5/6 as a distinct document-control/effectivity unit from Section 7 (`67-16127`, 1 July 1967).
- When `67-14188` is recovered, inspect title/control/change pages before using technical content.
- No station maturity or executable behavior changes from this bibliographic result.
