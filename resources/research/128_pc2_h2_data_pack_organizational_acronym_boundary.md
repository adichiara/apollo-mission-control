# 128 — PC+2 H-2 data-pack organizational-acronym boundary

Date: 2026-09-14

## Question

Can the surviving Apollo-era organization records safely expand any of the PHO-TR155 H-2 data-pack labels listed in PHO-TR460, or otherwise narrow what those labels represented?

## Primary evidence

### PHO-TR460 — H-2 pack labels

Philco-Ford's contemporaneous progress report lists H-2 PHO-TR155 data-pack products using the labels `FDB`, `CSB`, `FCOB`, `LSB`, `SLV`, `FSB`, `ESB`, `MOD`, `PCB`, and `OSB`. It does not expand those labels in the reviewed passage.

Primary source:

- Philco-Ford, **PHO-TR460**, NASA NTRS 19690029816: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19690029816/downloads/19690029816.pdf?attachment=true

### MSC organization records — contemporary branch names

The Manned Spacecraft Center telephone directory for November 1970 identifies Flight Control Division organizations including:

- FC2 — **Flight Control Operations Branch**;
- FC3 — **CSM Systems Branch**;
- FC4 — **LM Systems Branch**;
- FC5 — **Flight Dynamics Branch**;
- FC7 — **Mission Control Requirements Branch**;
- FC9 — **Experiments Systems Branch**.

A separate Apollo-era ALSEP Flight Control Experiments Operations Plan acronym appendix explicitly uses:

- **FCOB — Flight Control Operations Branch**;
- **LSB — LM Systems Branch**.

Primary sources:

- Manned Spacecraft Center, **Telephone Directory, November 1970**, organizational mail-code listing (Flight Control Division).
- NASA/MSC, **ALSEP Flight Control Experiments Operations Plan**, acronym appendix, archived by the Lunar and Planetary Institute.

## Finding

The new evidence establishes that at least `FCOB` and `LSB` were genuine contemporary Apollo flight-control organizational acronyms for **Flight Control Operations Branch** and **LM Systems Branch**. The same organization record also shows branch names whose initials resemble several other PHO-TR460 pack labels (`CSB`, `FDB`, `ESB`).

That resemblance is useful for archival searching, but it is not sufficient to expand the PHO-TR460 pack labels. The reviewed primary sources do not explicitly state that the H-2 pack label `CSB` means CSM Systems Branch, `FDB` means Flight Dynamics Branch, or `ESB` means Experiments Systems Branch; nor do they define `SLV`, `FSB`, `MOD`, `PCB`, or `OSB` in the PHO-TR155 data-pack context.

The correct historical boundary is therefore:

`contemporary organization acronyms/names` **may guide search terms** → `PHO-TR460 pack-label meaning` **remains unproven until a pack key, transmittal, or explicit cross-reference is recovered**.

## Historical boundary

The repository may state that:

- `FCOB` was a contemporary Apollo acronym for Flight Control Operations Branch;
- `LSB` was a contemporary Apollo acronym for LM Systems Branch;
- Flight Control Division also contained CSM Systems, Flight Dynamics, Mission Control Requirements, and Experiments Systems branches in the same era;
- several PHO-TR460 pack labels are therefore plausible organizationally derived labels and these names are useful archival search terms.

The repository must not state, without a direct PHO-TR155/data-pack cross-reference, that:

- the PHO-TR460 `FCOB` or `LSB` pack necessarily consisted of material owned by those branches merely because the acronyms match;
- `CSB`, `FDB`, or `ESB` are proven expansions of the data-pack labels;
- `SLV`, `FSB`, `MOD`, `PCB`, or `OSB` have any particular expansion or content;
- any one branch-specific pack contained TELMU console-09 loading or `GC0071V` / `GC0155F`.

## First-playable consequence

No simulator or UI change is justified. This result improves archival search precision only. It does not recover the H-2 TELMU loading, exact inverter presentation, or sample cadence.

## Next archival target

Search should now combine the known pack labels with the contemporary organization names while still prioritizing direct primary artifacts:

1. Mission H-2 data-pack Revision N or its transmittal/index;
2. PHO-TR155 data-pack definition/key or distribution list;
3. H-2 Revision-C working lists tied to Flight Control Operations, LM Systems, CSM Systems, Flight Dynamics, or Experiments Systems;
4. Mission H-2 TELMU console-09 loading material;
5. Mission H-2 TDFCB Revision 4 special LM Flight Control / PCMGS listings.
