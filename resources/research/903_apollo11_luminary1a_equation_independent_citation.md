# 903 — Apollo 11 LUMINARY 1A equation document independent citation

Research thread: `apollo11-descent-runtime`

## Bounded question

Can a mission-era primary source independently confirm that the May 1969 *Programmed Guidance Equations for LUMINARY 1A* document was an operational reference for Mission G, rather than relying only on the later LUMINARY 1B reissue?

## Findings

Yes. NASA/MSC's Mission G rendezvous documentation includes, in its reference list, *Programmed Guidance Equations For Luminary 1A Manned LM Earth Orbital and Lunar Program*, Flight Software Branch, Flight Support Division, dated May 1969.

This independently corroborates the title, issuing organization, date, and Mission G relevance of the LUMINARY 1A programmed-guidance-equation document. Research 902 remains the authority for the document-number relationship: MSC-69-FS-4 says it is a complete reissue of MSC-69-FS-3 updated for LUMINARY 1B.

The new source does **not** reproduce the 69-FS-3 body or establish exact equation text. A focused public search again failed to recover an authenticated copy of 69-FS-3.

## Runtime consequence

The source boundary is now stronger:

- 69-FS-3 is independently corroborated as a May 1969 Mission G-era Flight Software Branch reference;
- 69-FS-4 remains usable as the primary 1A→1B change-control map;
- changed 1B pages still cannot be imported as Apollo-11-effective equations without 1A-effective evidence;
- the checkpoint/event runtime remains admissible without reconstructing unavailable exact equations.

## Recovery lead

The Neil A. Armstrong papers finding aid at Purdue catalogs adjacent Mission G guidance material, including Apollo Project Memo 7-69, *What is LUMINARY 1A?* (30 Jan 1969), and *Guidance, Navigation and Control Lunar Module Functional Description and Operation Using Flight Program Luminary (Rev. 069)* (5 Jun 1969). These are archival retrieval leads, not substitutes for 69-FS-3.

## Sources

- **DOCUMENTED:** MSC-69-FS-4 identifies that predecessor as MSC-69-FS-3 and describes the 1A→1B reissue/change relationship.
