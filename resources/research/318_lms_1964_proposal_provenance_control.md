# Research note 318 — 1964 LMS proposal provenance control

Date: 2026-09-18  
Status: **ORIGINAL CONTRACTOR PROPOSAL METADATA RECOVERED; DESIGN-BASELINE BOUNDARY TIGHTENED.**

## Question

Can the early LMS mathematical-model proposal already identified in the source catalog be placed under exact document/procurement control before its equations are used as evidence for the causal engine?

## Primary-source result

The surviving original proposal scan identifies itself as:

- **Proposal for LEM Mission Simulator**;
- **Volume II — Technical Addendum: Glossary of Symbols**;
- Link Group, Systems Division, General Precision Inc., Binghamton, New York;
- **Proposal No. 449**;
- **13 April 1964**;
- prepared for **Grumman Aircraft Engineering Corp., Bethpage, Long Island, New York**;
- furnished in response to **RFP NOLVR-440-43100**.

The proposal's own restrictive-data legend says the material was furnished to evaluate the proposal and distinguishes that evaluation use from rights that would follow if a contract were awarded. That is important evidence control: this scan is an original proposal artifact, not proof that every proposed equation, interface, implementation choice, or configuration was accepted into the delivered LMS.

## Consequence

The document remains valuable for recovering the *proposed* LMS mathematical-model vocabulary and flow architecture. It should be used as a baseline/design-lineage source and as a retrieval aid for later accepted documentation, especially the 1965 LMS math-model reports and the 1967 Instructor's Handbook.

It must not be promoted directly to Apollo 13 historical configuration. Any equation, constant, subsystem boundary, update rate, machine assignment, or interface taken from Proposal 449 requires later accepted/operational corroboration.

## Relationship to current open gates

This does not replace the current highest-priority retrieval task: direct page verification of the 1967 `LMA-790-2-LMS` Volume I candidate against MSC accession `67-14186` and its conflicting 1 April/15 May 1967 issue-date metadata.

It does improve provenance discipline for the parallel causal-engine source thread: comparisons can now explicitly distinguish **1964 proposed design** from **1965 math-model documentation**, **1967 instructor documentation**, and **1971 operational/user documentation**.

## Simulator/station consequence

None. No station maturity, executable constant, model equation, processor allocation, program assignment, or Apollo 13 profile changes.
