# Research note 234 — RG 255 E.155B visual-material provenance boundary

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: **primary archival provenance clarified; technical contents still unrecovered**

## Question

Should E.155B be treated as an LMS visual-system project-file series comparable to E.155B1, or only as an archival location containing some LMS MEP/EVDE material?

## Primary source

NASA/NARA, *Preliminary Inventory of the Records of the National Aeronautics and Space Administration, Record Group 255*, series E.155B and E.155B1.

Primary scan: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARA-SW/Rg255-1.pdf

## Finding

The inventory identifies **E.155B as “Reference Files on the Lunar Landing Training Vehicle,” 1962–1973, 20 ft.** Its description says the series contains LLTV/LLRV correspondence, design reports/specifications, procurement/program plans, schedules, readiness-review material, flight-test material, procedures, accident-investigation records, photographs, drawings, tapes, film, and contractor reports. Only then does it state that there is **“also some material relating to”** the Lunar Module mission simulator **Mission Effects Projector (MEP) and External Visual Display Equipment (EVDE)**.

By contrast, **E.155B1 is explicitly “Project Files on the Lunar Module Simulator,” 1968–1970, 2 ft.** It is the dedicated LMS series containing correspondence, statements of work, procurement/program plans, acceptance test plans/procedures, technical reports, operations manuals, surface-model photographs, and drawings; most correspondence is described as Kollsman/NAS9-8634 administration for construction of LM simulators at MSC and KSC.

## Consequence

E.155B is a valid direct archival location for MEP/EVDE evidence, but it must **not** be described or searched as though the whole series were an LMS visual-system project file. Its MEP/EVDE holdings are an explicitly identified subset embedded in LLTV reference files apparently maintained by Warren North/Aircraft Operations for reference.

This changes retrieval tactics:

1. Request E.155B narrowly by **MEP / Mission Effects Projector / EVDE / External Visual Display Equipment / Lunar Module mission simulator**, plus old accessions `72A797` and `73ABC1675`, rather than requesting the 20-foot series as an LMS body of records.
2. Keep E.155B1 / `72A794` as the first dedicated LMS contract/configuration retrieval target.
3. Do not infer a technical, contractual, organizational, or configuration relationship between E.155B's MEP/EVDE subset and the surrounding LLTV/LLRV material merely from archival co-location.

## Not established

This inventory-level clarification does not establish MEP/EVDE hardware design, vendor, requirements, signal mapping, acceptance criteria, relationship to `NAS9-8634`, relationship to Farrand/Itek, Mission Control routing, or Apollo 13 effectivity.
