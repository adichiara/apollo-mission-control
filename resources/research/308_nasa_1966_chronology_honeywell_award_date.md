# Research 308 — NASA 1966 chronology corroborates Honeywell award date

Date: 2026-09-18

## Question

Can the 21 July versus 21 September 1966 Honeywell simulator-computer award discrepancy be narrowed without treating an uninspected archival folder title as evidence?

## Result

Yes, but not fully closed. NASA's *Astronautics and Aeronautics, 1966: Chronology on Science, Technology, and Policy* independently records the Honeywell award under **21 September 1966**. Its entry states that NASA awarded Honeywell, Inc. a $4.2-million fixed-price contract for **six computer complexes** supporting Apollo command-module and lunar-module simulators at MSC and KSC, with completion by March 1967, and explicitly cites **NASA Release 66-254**.

This is independent NASA-published corroboration of the date/citation already recovered through SP-4009. It makes 21 September 1966 the strongly corroborated NASA chronology date for the award and further isolates Tomayko's 21 July 1966 date as the discrepant value.

## Evidence class

This volume is an official NASA chronology compiled close to the events and explicitly points to the contemporaneous primary record, NASA Release `66-254`. It is stronger corroboration than later secondary retellings, but it is **not a substitute for inspection of Release 66-254 itself or the underlying contract**.

Accordingly, the project may say that two NASA chronology publications independently place the award on 21 September 1966 and cite Release 66-254. It should not yet claim that the July date has been disproved by the underlying contracting record.

## Important boundary

- The entry says **six computer complexes**; do not convert this to six DDP-224 processors.
- It does not define the processor count inside each complex, machine/program ownership, site allocation, acceptance configuration, or Apollo 13 effectivity.
- The original Release `66-254` remains unrecovered in inspected form.
- Wichita State `MS 87-08`, Box 40 FF 90 remains a retrieval lead only until its contents are inspected.

## Source

NASA, *Astronautics and Aeronautics, 1966: Chronology on Science, Technology, and Policy*, entry for 21 September 1966, p. 298. NASA-hosted PDF: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/1966.pdf

The entry explicitly cites `NASA Release 66-254`.
