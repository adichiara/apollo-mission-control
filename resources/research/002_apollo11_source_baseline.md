# Research Note 002 — Apollo 11 Source Baseline

**Date:** 2026-09-11  
**Status:** REVIEWED-PARTIAL

## Purpose

Assess whether Apollo 11 has enough primary documentation to remain a strong candidate for the first simulation baseline.

## Confirmed source coverage

### Mission rules

NASA preserves the Apollo 11 Flight Mission Rules for AS-506/CSM-107/LM-5. The document states that the rules represented Apollo 11 mission rules as of 1969-04-16 and were to receive extensive evaluation during premission simulations.

This provides mission-specific decision criteria rather than generic Apollo doctrine.

Source:
https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/a11missionrules.pdf

### Detailed flight plan

NASA preserves preliminary and final Apollo 11 flight plans. The archive identifies the final version as dated 1969-07-01 and prepared by the Flight Planning Branch, Flight Crew Support Division.

The flight plan includes detailed mission elapsed-time timelines, update forms, objective activities, and consumables analysis.

Source index:
https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/a11-fltplan.html

### Phase-level access

NASA's Lunar Surface Journal provides individual flight-plan pages for the LM-intensive interval, including:

- LM activation
- PGNS/AGS tests
- radar/radio checkout
- undocking and separation
- DOI
- PDI/touchdown
- pre-ascent checks
- ascent
- rendezvous
- docking

Source:
https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a11/plans11.html

### Flight-control manning information

NASA-TM-103373 includes an official Apollo 11 flight-control manning list.

Source:
https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19910008862

## Current conclusion

Apollo 11 remains a **strong candidate**, but it is **not selected yet**.

Before selection, determine whether enough documentation exists for:

- controller-specific displays during the target interval
- controller responsibilities and backroom support
- relevant telemetry parameters
- integrated-simulation malfunction cases
- voice-loop material

The project should not select Apollo 11 merely because the mission is famous.

## Candidate interval worth researching

The interval around LM activation → undocking → DOI → powered descent is documentation-rich and spans several controller disciplines. This is a research priority, not yet a design decision.
