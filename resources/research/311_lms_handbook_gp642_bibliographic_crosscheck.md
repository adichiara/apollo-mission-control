# Research 311 — LMS handbook GP-642 bibliographic cross-check

Date: 2026-09-18

## Question

Can another NASA-controlled bibliography independently constrain the date of `LMA-790-2-LMS` while the handbook text itself remains unrecovered?

## Primary-source result

NASA publication `GP-642`, a NASA scientific-and-technical reports bibliography, independently catalogs the Lunar Module Mission Simulator Instructor's Handbook under contract `NAS9-1100` and gives the handbook date as **1 April 1967**.

Research 310 recorded a NASA/MSC corporate-author index that assigns `67-14186` to Volume I, *Simulator Description*, and `67-14187` to Volume II, *Simulator Operation Sections 1 and 4*, but prints **15 May 1967**. GP-642 therefore provides a second NASA bibliographic-control path supporting the earlier April date already present in NASA indexing.

## Evidence boundary

This is bibliographic evidence only. It does **not** recover handbook technical content or establish DDP-224 machine count/workload, LMS site topology, Apollo 13 effectivity, or software ownership. It also does not explain whether 1 April and 15 May represent publication, receipt, cataloging, revision, or another document-control event. The discrepancy remains open.

## Retrieval consequence

Direct recovery remains the gate. Use `67-14186` first for Volume I and `67-14187` for Volume II Sections 1 and 4. Archival requests should include both dates and `NAS9-1100` so a catalog-date mismatch does not produce a false negative.

## Status

Bibliographic cross-check complete. Technical-content recovery remains open.
