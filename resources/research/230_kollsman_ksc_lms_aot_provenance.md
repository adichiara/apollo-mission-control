# Research note 230 — Kollsman hardware provenance in the KSC Lunar Module Simulator

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: **Kollsman-manufactured flight-representative hardware is directly tied to the KSC LMS; NAS9-8634 subsystem scope remains unresolved**

## Question

Can an authoritative collection record independently connect Kollsman hardware to an actual Apollo Lunar Module Simulator, without inferring that `NAS9-8634` covered the Mission Effects Projector or External Visual Display Equipment?

## Authoritative collection result

The Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum object record for **Alignment Optical Telescope, Lunar Module Simulator** (`A19751446001`) states that the functioning AOT was originally part of the Lunar Module Simulator used for astronaut mission training at Kennedy Space Center. The record identifies the **manufacturer as Kollsman Instrument Company** and the **designer as MIT Instrumentation Laboratory**. It further states that the LMS, including the telescope, was transferred to the Smithsonian in 1974, remained stored at KSC for nearly 25 years, and that the AOT was later removed from the simulator crew compartment for conservation; a replica was supplied for the LMS display.

Source: https://www.si.edu/object/alignment-optical-telescope-lunar-module-simulator%3Anasm_A19751446001

## What this establishes

This is independent object-level provenance for a specific Kollsman-manufactured component physically associated with the **KSC Lunar Module Simulator**. It strengthens the general Kollsman/KSC LMS relationship beyond the RG 255 E.155B1 series description.

It also supplies a useful archival/object identifier: `A19751446001`.

## Evidence boundary

The Smithsonian record does **not** establish that:

- the AOT was procured under `NAS9-8634`;
- Kollsman was the prime LMS contractor rather than the manufacturer of this specific optical subsystem;
- `NAS9-8634` covered the AOT, MEP, EVDE, visual scene generation, or any other particular subsystem;
- Kollsman replaced Farrand as MEP supplier;
- the AOT provenance identifies the configuration used for Apollo 13 training;
- any AOT signal was represented in `LMA-790-2-LMS` Section 7 or routed to a Mission Control station.

The record therefore **must not** be used to collapse the Kollsman AOT and Farrand MEP/EVDE trails into one procurement or vendor-succession story.

## Retrieval consequence

The next E.155B1 search should add the terms **AOT**, **alignment optical telescope**, **optical subsystem**, and KSC, alongside `NAS9-8634` and Kollsman. Recovered statements of work, procurement plans, acceptance plans, drawings, or correspondence can then determine whether the archival contract files concern this known Kollsman LMS hardware, other simulator construction, or both.

Continue E.155B MEP/EVDE retrieval separately. Only explicit document text should connect the two workstreams.
