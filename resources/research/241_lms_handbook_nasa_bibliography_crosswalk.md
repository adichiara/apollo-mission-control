# Research note 241 — LMS handbook NASA bibliography crosswalk

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: **official bibliography closes report-number/title/date crosswalk; digitized-scan identity still gated**

## Question

Can an independent primary NASA bibliography tighten the identity crosswalk between the Virtual AGC digitized LMS handbook candidate and the `LMA-790-2-LMS` / MSC accession family without assuming that a matching web title is the accession copy?

## Primary-source-first result

NASA/KSC bibliography GP-642 contains an explicit entry for the handbook. Item 346 identifies:

- Grumman Aircraft Engineering Corporation;
- **LUNAR MODULE MISSION SIMULATOR INSTRUCTORS HANDBOOK**;
- Volume I — **Simulator Description**;
- Volume II — Section 1, **Simulator Operation**, and Section 4, **Instructor Activity for Interface Operation**;
- date **Apr. 1, 1967**;
- Grumman report **GAEC LMA 790-2-LMS, Vols. I and II**.

This is independent primary NASA bibliographic confirmation that the exact Volume I title surfaced by the Virtual AGC collection belongs to the `LMA-790-2-LMS` report family and that NASA/KSC cataloged the combined Volume I / Volume II Sections 1 and 4 material under the 1 April 1967 date.

The MSC corporate-author index independently maps that report family to `NAS9-1100` and accession `67-14186` for Volume I, with `67-14187`, `67-14188`, and `67-16127` covering the Volume II sections.

## What this closes

The title/report-number crosswalk is no longer dependent on title similarity alone:

`Lunar Module Mission Simulator Instructors Handbook, Volume I — Simulator Description` → `GAEC LMA 790-2-LMS` is explicitly supported by NASA GP-642.

GP-642 also independently supports **1 April 1967** as a NASA-cataloged date for this report family, strengthening that side of the date discrepancy recorded in note 240.

## What this does not close

GP-642 does **not** prove that the particular Virtual AGC PDF is the physical/digitized copy cataloged as MSC accession `67-14186`. The scan itself still must expose sufficient title/revision/provenance evidence before it is treated as that accession copy.

Do not infer from this bibliography:

- DDP-224 workload allocation;
- common-memory mapping;
- program/model ownership;
- site-specific configuration;
- Apollo 13 effectivity;
- Mission Control routing;
- simulation cadence or numerical tolerances.

## Date discrepancy

The evidence now stands as:

- MSC main corporate-author index: `01-04-67` for `67-14186` / `67-14187`;
- NASA/KSC GP-642: **Apr. 1, 1967** for `LMA 790-2-LMS`, Vols. I and II;
- MSC supplement: `15-05-67` for `67-14186` / `67-14187`.

Two primary NASA bibliographic controls therefore support 1 April 1967, but the 15 May entry is not discarded or reinterpreted without revision evidence.
