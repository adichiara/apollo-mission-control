# Research note 164 — PC+2 Flight Director Log OCR route

Date: 2026-09-15

## Question

Can the primary handwritten Flight Director log identified in note 163 be made practically searchable for the unresolved PC+2 mass-properties/trim decision?

## Finding

Yes. Virtual AGC/ibiblio's Apollo Documents directory exposes a separate file named **`00 A13 Flight Director Log - OCR.pdf`**, listed as 231 MB and dated 2022-07-08. The Apollo 13 Flight Journal independently catalogs the complete high-resolution handwritten Flight Director Log (378 MB) and states the archival provenance of the high-resolution collection.

Primary/high-resolution collection index:
`https://apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/a13-documents.html`

OCR derivative directory:
`https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/`

## Interpretation

This resolves the *access/search-method* portion of the previous blocker. The research path is now:

1. search the OCR derivative for candidate pages/terms in the ~59–74 GET interval;
2. use OCR only to locate likely material;
3. verify every substantive reading against the handwritten high-resolution page image;
4. record only verified content as historical evidence.

Likely search terms remain PC+2, trim, GDA, gimbal, mass properties, weight, CG, CONTROL, Flight Dynamics, RTCC, RTACF, job, and run.

## Evidence boundary

No new PC+2 trim value, tolerance, job number, or controller rationale is claimed here. The OCR derivative is a search aid, not an authoritative transcription. Handwritten OCR can omit or corrupt exactly the terse numerical annotations sought in this investigation.

## Consequence for simulator research

The unresolved item remains the controller-side calculation/comparison that justified the explicit no-PC+2-trim ground rule. The next research action should use the OCR copy to identify candidate Flight Director Log pages and then validate them against the archival scan before changing the numerical model.
