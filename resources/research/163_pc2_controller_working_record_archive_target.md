# 163 — PC+2 controller working-record archive target

## Question

Can the next primary-source search be narrowed from a generic request for a PC+2 trim calculation to a concrete surviving controller record likely to preserve the decision path behind the documented no-trim ground rule?

## Primary-source discovery

The Apollo 13 Flight Journal mission-document index now exposes a high-resolution scan of the **Apollo 13 Flight Director Log**, credited to scans acquired from the University of Houston–Clear Lake / National Archives–Fort Worth collections. The index describes the high-resolution collection as scans of original files and separately lists the complete Flight Director Log as a 378 MB PDF.

Document index:
`https://apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/a13-documents.html`

Flight Director Log scan linked there:
`https://apollojournals.org/afj/ap13fj/pdf-hr/fdlog/01-a13-fdlog.pdf`

This is materially different from the already-used Flight Director **mission narrative** in the Flight Control Division Mission Operations Report. The log is the surviving handwritten operational record and is therefore the highest-priority primary artifact for the unresolved controller-side PC+2 comparison/decision.

## Corroborating archival provenance

The Virtual AGC document-library change log independently identifies the Apollo 13 Flight Director log as the flight directors' handwritten logs throughout the mission and credits the University of Houston–Clear Lake archives for access. This is useful provenance/navigation evidence, but the historical claims must come from the scanned log itself once the relevant pages are inspected.

## What this establishes

This run does **not** recover a candidate trim, tolerance, job number, or exact decision time. It does establish that the project should stop treating a Flight Director log page as a hypothetical archival target: a complete digitized scan is now known and directly addressable.

The unresolved chain remains:

`T+55 LM-burn deck family -> PC+2 calculation/run -> candidate/reference trim comparison -> controller disposition -> explicit no-trim ground rule -> V34 after N47 -> no N48 entry`.

The next evidence-bearing action is page-level inspection of the Flight Director Log across the post-free-return / pre-PC+2 interval, especially the ~59–74 GET controller handovers and PC+2 preparation pages, for handwritten references to mass properties, trim, GDA, CONTROL, Flight Dynamics, or a job/run identifier.

## Evidence boundary

- Do not infer that the Flight Director Log necessarily contains the missing calculation.
- Do not cite auction descriptions or modern summaries as evidence for PC+2 numerical values.
- Do not revive the superseded search for a mandatory “final Noun 48 pair”; notes 158–162 establish that the final workflow deliberately terminated before N48.
- The scan is image-heavy and must be inspected page-by-page; absence cannot be claimed until the relevant interval is actually reviewed.

## Model consequence

No numerical model field changes in this note. Add an archival-provenance field/source pointer for the controller decision record and preserve all unresolved comparison fields as unknown.
