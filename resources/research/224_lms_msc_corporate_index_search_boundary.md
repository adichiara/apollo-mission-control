# Research note 224 — LMS MSC corporate-index search boundary

Date: 2026-09-17  
Status: **PRIMARY INDEX SEARCH COMPLETED; no accession for `1L5-102-(H)` recovered.**

## Question

Can the NASA Manned Spacecraft Center corporate-author index supply an MSC accession/report trail for General Precision/Link acceptance procedure `1L5-102-(H)` or purchase order `2-24481C`, reducing dependence on the secondary surviving-manual catalog?

## Primary-source result

The NASA Manned Spacecraft Center *Corporate Author Index of Apollo Technical Documentation* (AETI) is a primary NASA index of Apollo technical documentation. Its introduction states that the AETI series was intended to index all technical documentation relating to the Apollo program that had been formally transmitted to MSC, with entries grouped by corporate author and keyed by accession/report/contract information.

The index contains a distinct **LINK GROUP, GENERAL PRECISION INC.** corporate-author section. The currently searchable OCR exposes Link entries for Apollo Mission Simulator material, demonstrating that Link/General Precision documentation could be indexed under that corporate author.

Targeted searches of this primary index for `1L5-102`, `L5-102`, `2-24481C`, and `MODIFICATION KIT` did **not** recover the LMS acceptance procedure or a matching accession.

Primary source: NASA MSC, *Corporate Author Index of Apollo Technical Documentation* (AETI), scan hosted in the NARA Apollo collection:
https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARASWoverflow/CorporateIndex.pdf

## Interpretation boundary

This is a **negative retrieval result, not evidence that the report was never transmitted to MSC or did not exist**. OCR can miss report identifiers; AETI coverage is limited to documentation formally transmitted and indexed by MSC; and the surviving `1L5-102-(H)` identity still comes from a secondary catalog until the manual is directly inspected.

Do not infer acceptance criteria, Modification Kit No. 1 scope, simulator effectivity, or Apollo 13 applicability from the absence of an AETI hit.

## Project consequence

The AETI route does not currently supply the missing accession. Retrieval priority therefore remains:

1. RG 255 E.155B1 LMS acceptance/correlation holdings;
2. direct inspection of surviving `1L5-102-(H)` or another archival copy;
3. Grumman/Link holdings keyed by `2-24481C`, `Section E, Paragraph 5`, and report-number variants;
4. direct recovery of LMA-790-2-LMS Volume II Section 7 for the output-side crosswalk.

The primary-index negative result should prevent repeated searches from being mistaken for a still-untried path while preserving the possibility of OCR/indexing failure.
