# Research 309 — MCC-H simulation-computer acquisition archival crosswalk

Date: 2026-09-18

## Question

Does Wichita State `MS 87-08`, Box 40 FF 91 belong to the unresolved LMS DDP-224 procurement trail, and can its underlying record be identified without inferring from the folder title?

## Result

The archival item can be crosswalked to a separately cited MSC record, and it is an **MCC-H simulation-computer acquisition record rather than an LMS DDP-224 procurement record**.

Wichita State catalogs Box 40 FF 91 as *Letter to General Frank A. Bogart from Assistant Director for Flight Operations. Recommended Change to the Mission Control Center-Houston (MCC-H) Apollo Simulation Computer Acquisition Plan.*

NASA/JSC's HAER documentation for Apollo Mission Control identifies the underlying record more precisely as Christopher C. Kraft, Jr., *Recommended Change to the Mission Control Center-Houston (MCC-H) Apollo Simulation Computer Acquisition Plan*, **13 January 1966**, Apollo Program Chronological Files, **Box 066-44**, Johnson Space Center History Collection, University of Houston-Clear Lake. The HAER text reports that Kraft sought additional MCC simulation capability and specifically recommended **five IBM 360 computers**.

## Evidence class

The Wichita finding aid is archival metadata. The HAER report is a NASA/JSC-sponsored later historical documentation project that supplies an exact citation and a limited quotation/synthesis of the 1966 Kraft memorandum. The 13 January 1966 memorandum itself has **not** been directly inspected in this pass, so its full contents must not be represented as recovered primary evidence.

This crosswalk is nevertheless useful retrieval control: it identifies the author, date, title, and JSC History Collection location of the primary record and shows that FF 91 concerns MCC-H simulation computing, not the Honeywell DDP-224 LMS/CMS complex procurement.

## Important boundary

Do not use FF 91 or the HAER summary to infer:

- LMS or CMS DDP-224 processor counts;
- Honeywell contract terms or the 21 Jul./21 Sep. 1966 date discrepancy;
- any relationship between the requested five IBM 360s and the six Honeywell simulator computer complexes;
- deployed LMS machine/program ownership;
- Apollo 13 LMS effectivity.

The systems and procurement questions are distinct unless a primary record explicitly connects them.
