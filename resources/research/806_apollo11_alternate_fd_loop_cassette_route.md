# Research note 806 — Apollo 11 alternate FD-loop cassette route

Date: 2026-09-24
Research thread: `apollo11-descent-audio-provenance`

## Question

Does primary NASA catalog evidence provide a provenance-bearing Flight Director-loop retrieval route independent of the unresolved `792-AAI` DVIDS page-to-reel mapping?

## Sources

- NASA, *Historical Audio Archive — Digital Audio File Metadata*: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/NASA-Audio-Archive_Digital-Audio-File_Metadata.pdf
- Honeysuckle Creek, *Apollo 11 Descent & Landing*: https://www.honeysucklecreek.net/msfn_missions/MSFN/Network_A11_landing.html — secondary retrieval/restoration lead only.

## Findings

NASA's historical-audio metadata catalogs `963-AAE` as **Apollo 11 FD Loop Cassette — AOS Through After Landing** and `964-AAE` as **Apollo 11 FD Loop Cassette — Liftoff — Lunar Descent — Lunar Ascent**. It also catalogs `550-AAG` as landing audio containing Flight Director and air-to-ground.

This closes a narrow retrieval question: direct descent FD-loop work does not depend exclusively on resolving the public DVIDS mapping for `792-AAI`. The NASA catalog itself supplies other named Apollo 11 FD-loop assets whose described scope includes the landing/descent interval.

The catalog descriptions do **not** establish that `792-AAI`, `963-AAE`, `964-AAE`, or `550-AAG` are copies of one recording, share a loop mix, use a common time zero, or have sample-exact GMT/GET alignment. No dialogue or speaker attribution is promoted from catalog metadata.

The secondary Honeysuckle Creek reconstruction reports that JSC supplied 963/964 as overlapping FD-loop recordings and describes restoration/synchronization work. That is useful for locating candidate audio, but its timing, edits, speaker labels, and inferred recording circumstances remain secondary until checked against a primary NASA/JSC asset.

## Unresolved

1. Recover a provenance-bearing NASA/JSC digital `963-AAE` or `550-AAG` asset.
2. Independently align file elapsed time to primary GMT/GET anchors.
3. Inspect the five Mission Report 1201/1202 windows.
4. Compare the FD-loop result with Historical Recorder #1 GUIDO L/R channels 21/22.
5. Keep exact Mission-G station/keyset/display configuration blocked on PHO-TN401 or equivalent evidence.

## Evidence status

- **DOCUMENTED:** NASA catalog identity and descent/landing relevance of `963-AAE`, `964-AAE`, and `550-AAG`.
- **UNRESOLVED:** digital-asset provenance/retrieval, exact clock alignment, direct alarm-window transcription, inter-recording relationship, and speaker attribution.
