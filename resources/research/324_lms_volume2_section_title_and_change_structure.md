# Research note 324 — LMS Volume II section titles and changed-issue structure

Date: 2026-09-18

## Question

Can the archival description resolve the individual subjects of `LMA-790-2-LMS` Volume II Sections 5 and 6 and clarify how the surviving April/May 1967 material is arranged without inferring unsupported revision history?

## Archival-source result

The current finding aid for the **James J. Avitabile Papers, Ms2001-057**, Special Collections and University Archives, Virginia Tech, describes the LMS handbook material as follows:

- Volume I — **Simulator Description**;
- Volume II — **Simulator Operation**, 1 April 1967;
- Section 1 — **Simulator Operation** (2 folders);
- Volume II — **Simulator Operation (Changed)**, 15 May 1967;
- Section 1 — **Simulator Operation**;
- Section 2 — **Malfunction Data** (3 folders);
- Section 3 — **Lunar-landing Mission Procedures** (4 folders);
- Section 4 — **Instructor Activity for Interface Operation**;
- a second Section 4 holding with the same title;
- Volume II — **Simulator Operation**, 1 April 1967;
- Section 5 — **Instructor Material** (6 folders);
- Section 6 — **Scripting Data Sheets** (2 folders);
- Section 7 — **Simulator Output Tables**, 1 July 1967 (3 folders).

Finding aid: https://arvasarchive.org/catalog/viblbv_repositories_2_resources_2187

This resolves the previously unknown individual subjects of Sections 5 and 6 and supplies folder extents. It also provides archival evidence that a 15 May 1967 holding is explicitly described as **Changed**, while the April material is separately described.

## Cross-check against primary NASA/MSC control

NASA/MSC bibliographic control independently identifies:

- `67-14187`: Volume II Sections 1 and 4, 1 April 1967 in the original index and 15 May 1967 in the supplement;
- `67-14188`: Volume II Sections 5 and 6, 1 April 1967;
- `67-16127`: Volume II Section 7, **Simulator Output Tables**, 1 July 1967.

The finding-aid arrangement is therefore compatible with the recovered NASA issue-date records and gives the first repository-level evidence that the May material was considered a changed issue. It does **not** establish the technical nature, scope, or completeness of those changes.

## Interpretation boundary

The finding aid does not expose the handbook pages themselves. Therefore it does not establish:

- which exact pages or instructions changed on 15 May;
- whether Sections 2 or 3 belong to the April issue, May changed issue, or both;
- whether Section 4's duplicate holdings correspond one-for-one to April and May without page inspection;
- whether Sections 5/6 were later changed;
- Apollo 13 effectivity;
- individual malfunction definitions, scripting syntax, instructor procedures, output-variable semantics, processor allocation, numerical cadence, or executable constants.

Do not turn the archival word **Changed** into an assumed revision number or technical delta. Recover and compare the actual control/change pages and body pages first.

## Repository consequence

- Section 5 is now retrieval-controlled as **Instructor Material** (6 folders).
- Section 6 is now retrieval-controlled as **Scripting Data Sheets** (2 folders).
- Section 4 is retrieval-controlled as **Instructor Activity for Interface Operation**.
- The 15 May 1967 Volume II holding may now be described as an archival **Changed** issue, while the reason and technical delta remain unresolved.
- Section 2 remains the highest-value direct-content target for malfunction insertion; Section 6 becomes a specific target for recovering historical scripting abstractions.
- No station maturity, Apollo 13 effectivity, processor allocation, causal constant, or executable behavior changes from this result.
