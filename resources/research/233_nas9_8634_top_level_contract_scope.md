# Research note 233 — NAS9-8634 top-level contract scope

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: **primary top-level contract purpose established; subsystem scope remains unresolved**

## Question

What can the primary RG 255 inventory itself establish about `NAS9-8634`, without waiting for the E.155B1 files or inferring subsystem scope?

## Primary evidence

The NASA/NARA RG 255 preliminary inventory describes series **E.155B1 — Project Files on the Lunar Module Simulator, 1968–1970** and states that most correspondence is with Kollsman Instrument Corporation and relates to administration of **contract `NAS9-8634` for the construction of LM simulators at both MSC and KSC**.

The same series contains correspondence, memoranda, statement of work, program and procurement plans, acceptance test plans and procedures, progress and technical reports, operations manuals, photographs of surface models, and copies of drawings. The locator is old accession `72A794`, location `A-23-16-6`.

Primary source: NASA/NARA RG 255 preliminary inventory, p. 25: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARA-SW/Rg255-1.pdf

## What this establishes

`NAS9-8634` is not merely an unidentified Kollsman contract associated chronologically with LMS records. The primary archival description explicitly assigns it a top-level purpose: **construction of LM simulators at both MSC and KSC**.

This closes the repository's top-level contract-purpose question and gives the E.155B1 retrieval a firmer interpretation: the file series is project documentation for a Kollsman LMS construction contract spanning both simulator sites.

## Evidence boundary

The inventory description does **not** establish:

- how many simulator units or assemblies `NAS9-8634` covered;
- whether “construction” meant complete new simulators, modification/rebuild of existing simulators, specific assemblies, or another contractual implementation;
- which LMS subsystems were Kollsman's responsibility;
- whether MEP, EVDE, AOT, surface models, or other optical/visual equipment fell within the contract scope;
- whether Kollsman replaced Farrand or another vendor;
- the dated configuration or Apollo 13 effectivity of either site;
- any Mission Control output, interface, routing, or controller workflow.

Those questions still require file-level E.155B1/E.155B evidence.

Fresh exact/variant public searches for `NAS9-8634` with Kollsman and LMS terms did not recover a digitized NASA contract or statement of work that narrows the inventory's top-level description. This preserves the retrieval boundary recorded in research note 232.
