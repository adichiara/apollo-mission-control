# Research note 334 — Apollo 11 MCC display-usage source retrieval status

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: **BLOCKED — identified archival primary source is not digitally available**

## Question

Can the next unresolved Apollo 11 controller-display item — exact request behavior, display usage, or timing — be closed from the mission-specific Philco-Ford usage study rather than inferred from later Apollo material?

## Primary-source-first retrieval result

The mission-specific source is identified bibliographically as:

B. Costis, W. Ortolani, and W. Moreland, *NASA MCC Display/Control System Usage and Effectiveness, Apollo 11*, PHO-TN401, Philco-Ford Corporation, 24 December 1969.

A targeted public-web retrieval did not locate a digital copy. The Historic American Engineering Record (HAER) history for Johnson Space Center Apollo Mission Control cites the report to a physical archival holding: Box 078-65/66, Mission Documents: Apollo 11, Apollo Program, Johnson Space Center History Collection, University of Houston-Clear Lake.

The HAER report is useful as a government historical source and reports aggregate Apollo 11 display-request statistics from PHO-TN401, but it is not a substitute for inspecting PHO-TN401 itself when the question is exact station/request semantics, per-format behavior, or timing.

## Classification

The PHO-TN401 thread is **BLOCKED**, not merely unresolved or deferred. A discriminating primary source is known and its archival location is known, but the source text is not presently accessible digitally.

This does **not** put physical retrieval on the critical path for the current landing-radar estimator. It does put it on the evidence path for any claim that depends on mission-specific Apollo 11 display-request mechanics or usage timing beyond the aggregate figures already quoted by HAER.

## Consequence

Do not infer any of the following from the HAER aggregate statistics:

- a numeric CRT refresh period;
- per-field MSK-1137 update cadence;
- GUIDO keyboard/key sequence;
- display latency or stale-data threshold;
- which exact display formats GUIDO selected during powered descent.

The onboard 2-second landing-radar component cadence remains unrelated to this missing controller-display timing evidence.

## Next discriminating evidence

1. Retrieve PHO-TN401 from the Johnson Space Center History Collection at University of Houston-Clear Lake, or locate an authenticated digital scan.
2. Independently continue searching accessible mission-era Display/Control, RTCC, CCATS, console, and controller-procedure sources; those may close narrower routing/timing questions without PHO-TN401.
