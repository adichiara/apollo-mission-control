# Research note 231 — Kollsman AOT contract separation from LMS NAS9-8634 trail

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: **AOT program contract recovered; no documentary tie to NAS9-8634**

## Question

Does the known Kollsman Alignment Optical Telescope provenance support treating the AOT as part of Kollsman's `NAS9-8634` Lunar Module Simulator work?

## Primary-source result

NASA NTRS preserves Kollsman Instrument Corporation's February 1970 final-report set, *Apollo Optical Subsystem and LM Alignment Optical Telescope*:

- Volume I — NASA-CR-108358, NTRS `19700015843`, accession `70N25148`;
- Volume II, Part 1 — NASA-CR-108359, NTRS `19700015844`, accession `70N25149`;
- Volume II, Part 2 — NASA-CR-108360, NTRS `19700015845`, accession `70N25150`.

All three NTRS records identify funding contract **`NAS9-497`**. Volume I's original foreword states that the final report was prepared in conformance with the requirements of the NASA ACED contract `NAS 9-497`.

Primary sources:

- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19700015843
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19700015844
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19700015845
- https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700015843/downloads/19700015843.pdf

## What this establishes

Kollsman's Apollo optical-subsystem/AOT development had a directly documented contract identity: **NAS9-497**. This is distinct from the `NAS9-8634` contract number identified by the RG 255 E.155B1 series description for later Lunar Module Simulator correspondence.

This materially sharpens the retrieval boundary created in notes 229–230: the Smithsonian's Kollsman-manufactured AOT in the KSC LMS is evidence of hardware provenance, but the AOT program's own primary final report does not support assigning that hardware to `NAS9-8634`.

## Evidence boundary

Do **not** infer from the distinct contract numbers that:

- AOT hardware was never installed, maintained, modified, interfaced, or supported under another contract;
- `NAS9-8634` excluded all optical or guidance-related simulator work;
- `NAS9-8634` covered MEP/EVDE;
- Kollsman replaced Farrand as MEP supplier;
- the AOT final-report configuration is the exact Apollo 13 LMS training configuration;
- any AOT variable maps to Section 7 simulator outputs or a Mission Control station.

Those questions require explicit E.155B/E.155B1 or other primary contract/configuration records.

## Retrieval consequence

Treat **`NAS9-497` / AOT / Apollo Optical Subsystem** and **`NAS9-8634` / LMS project files** as separate contract trails unless a primary document explicitly links them. Continue E.155B1 retrieval using `NAS9-8634`, Kollsman, acceptance, statement of work, MEP, EVDE, surface model, and visual-system terms; use AOT terms only as a cross-reference rather than as an assumed `NAS9-8634` subsystem.
