# 334 — Apollo 11 DPS Supplement 7 identifier boundary

Date: 2026-09-22

## Question

Can the missing Apollo 11 Mission Report Supplement 7, *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation*, be assigned a defensible report number or accession from currently recoverable primary/catalog evidence?

## Primary/catalog evidence

Later NASA mission-report supplement tables establish that Apollo 11 Supplement 7 had the title *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation* and was published in **September 1970**.

The current NTRS record for Apollo 11 Supplement 5 demonstrates an important identifier caution. NTRS catalogs that sibling supplement under the parent mission-report number **MSC-00171**, plus `SUPPL-5`, `NASA-TM-X-68342`, and the older `MSC-PA-R-69-2`; it does not establish that every Apollo 11 supplement was cataloged under a simple constructed `MSC-00171-SUPPL-n` identifier.

NTRS's Apollo 12 DPS Supplement 5 record, by contrast, explicitly carries **MSC-01855-SUPPL-5**, **TRW-11176-H585-R0-00-SUPPL-5**, **NASA-TM-X-68933**, accession **73N15812**, and contract **NAS9-8166**. This confirms that DPS final-flight evaluations can have parallel MSC, TRW, NASA-TM, and accession identifiers, but the Apollo 12 identifiers are not transferable to Apollo 11.

A targeted search for `MSC-00171-SUPPL-7`, the title plus LM-5/TRW, and the title plus September 1970 did not recover an authoritative Apollo 11 Supplement 7 catalog record.

## Decision

Do **not** invent `MSC-00171-SUPPL-7` as the Apollo 11 DPS report number merely because later mission supplements use that pattern. Treat it only as a search string.

The next discriminating retrieval should target:

1. MSC/NASA bibliographic indexes around September 1970 using the parent report **MSC-00171**, title, and Supplement 7 together;
2. TRW Systems Group Apollo propulsion report indexes under contract **NAS9-8166**, without assuming a TRW report number;
3. archival catalog records capable of exposing legacy accession or NASA-TM crosswalks.

## Model consequence

None. No LM-5 thrust, FTP-force, mass-flow, or specific-impulse value is recovered by this pass.

## Sources

## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED / IDENTIFIER BLOCKED.** Existence, title, supplement number, and September 1970 publication are primary-documented. The Apollo 11 DPS report's own MSC/TRW/NASA-TM/accession identifiers and contents remain **BLOCKED ON BIBLIOGRAPHIC / ARCHIVAL RECOVERY**. Pattern-derived identifiers are explicitly rejected as evidence.
