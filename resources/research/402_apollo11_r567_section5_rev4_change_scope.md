# Research note 402 — R-567 Section 5 landing-change scope boundary

Date: 2026-09-19
Research thread: `apollo11-p66-pcr700`
Status: **Revision-5 landing-change inventory recovered; Apollo 11 equation effectivity still open**

## Question

Which surviving R-567 Section 5 control state documents the Apollo 11 landing changes PCR-670 and PCR-700?

## Findings

The MIT/IL R-567 Section 5 Revision 8 artifact preserves an earlier-change inventory under the explicit heading **Incorporated in Rev. 4 of GSOP**. The recovered list runs from PCR 254.2 through PCN 755 and includes PCR 636, *November, 1968 GSOP Section 5 Corrections in Landing Program*. It does **not** list PCR-670 or PCR-700 in that recovered Revision-4 inventory.

The surviving MIT/IL R-567 Section 5 cumulative artifact also preserves a historical revision-index sheet explicitly headed **Guidance Equations (Revision 5)**. On that sheet, PCR-670 (*Simplification of Landing Programs*) and PCR-700A (*Improve the Rate of Descent Mode (P66) Performance*) are both present and are not dagger-marked. The sheet legend marks daggered entries as material added in a later revision. This makes **Section 5 Revision 5 the earliest directly recovered Section 5 control state in this evidence chain that contains the two landing-change identifiers**. It does not prove that Revision 5 was the first historical documentation state, nor by itself establish Apollo 11 flight effectivity.

The separately controlled June 1969 R-567 Section 2 Revision 4 cover is explicitly for **LUMINARY 1A (Rev. 099)** and lists PCR-670 and PCR-700. LUMINARY Memo #67 independently records that PCR-670 and PCR-700 were implemented in LUMINARY Revision 72; Memo #73 later records the PCR-700 lag-compensation modification in Revision 80. These program records establish implementation ancestry, but do not substitute for the missing Section 5 page/effectivity chain.

The Section 5 sheet uses **PCR-700A**, while the Section 2 Rev. 4 control sheet and LUMINARY revision memos use **PCR-700**. The surviving document-library catalog describes PCR-700A as a rewritten clarification of PCR-700. That catalog description is a retrieval aid rather than primary evidence, so the repository does not silently equate every textual detail of 700 and 700A.

## Consequence

The next retrieval target is now **Section 5 Revision 5 and its P63–P66 affected pages**, not Revision 4. The controlled question is whether that Revision-5 page state can be tied to LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099/Apollo 11, and whether later cumulative copies preserve those equations unchanged. The missing `69-FS-3` remains the preferred parallel route to Apollo 11 programmed guidance equations.

No later equation text is promoted backward, and no executable or station-facing behavior changes.

## Evidence status

- **DOCUMENTED:** the recovered Section 5 Revision-4 inventory does not list PCR-670/PCR-700.
- **DOCUMENTED:** the surviving Section 5 Revision-5 index lists PCR-670 and PCR-700A without later-revision dagger marks.
- **DOCUMENTED:** Section 2 Revision 4, June 1969, explicitly names LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099 and lists PCR-670/PCR-700.
- **DOCUMENTED:** LUMINARY Memo #67 records implementation of PCR-670 and PCR-700 in program Revision 72; Memo #73 records the later PCR-700 lag-compensation change in Revision 80.
- **PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED:** Section 5 Revision 5 is the earliest directly recovered Section 5 control state in the currently inspected chain containing both landing-change identifiers; whether it was the first historical documentation state and whether its affected pages are Apollo 11 flight-effective remain unproven.
- **UNRESOLVED:** exact Apollo 11-effective P63–P66 pages/equations/cadence, a primary-source 700-to-700A textual/change-control crosswalk, and `69-FS-3` retrieval.
