# 141 — Apollo 13 DPS Supplement 2 contractor-provenance boundary

## Question

Can the published-but-unrecovered Apollo 13 Mission Report Supplement 2, *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation*, be narrowed further by contractor/report-family provenance without inventing a report number?

## Primary-source finding

NASA NTRS records establish a consistent TRW/Manned Spacecraft Center lineage for dedicated Apollo LM descent-propulsion final-flight evaluations around Apollo 13.

### Apollo 10 / LM-4

NTRS 19690026326 catalogs *Apollo 10 LM-4, descent propulsion system final flight evaluation* as a TRW Systems Group contractor report by R. K. M. Seto, published 8 August 1969 under contract `NAS9-8166`.

Verified identifiers:

- `TRW-11176-H314-R0-001`
- `NASA-CR-101869`

### Apollo 12

NTRS 19730078844 catalogs *Apollo 12 mission report: Descent propulsion system final flight evaluation (supplement 5)* by R. K. M. Seto and R. L. Barrows.

Verified identifiers:

- `TRW-11176-H585-R0-00-SUPPL-5`
- `MSC-01855-SUPPL-5`
- `NASA-TM-X-68933`
- contract `NAS9-8166`

### Apollo 14 / LM-8

NTRS 19730021081 catalogs *Apollo 14 mission report. Supplement 5: Descent propulsion system final flight evaluation* by A. T. Avvenire and S. C. Wood, TRW Systems Group.

Verified identifiers:

- `TRW-17618-H219-R0-00-SUPPL-5`
- `MSC-04112-SUPPL-5`
- `NASA-TM-X-69491`
- contract `NAS9-8166`

The Apollo 14 project-report scan independently shows the TRW Systems Group cover and the project technical report title.

Sources:

- NASA NTRS 19690026326: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19690026326
- NASA NTRS 19730078844: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730078844
- NASA NTRS 19730021081: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730021081
- Apollo 14 NASA scan: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a14/a14DPSPerformance.pdf

## What this establishes

The dedicated DPS final-flight-evaluation product family immediately before and after Apollo 13 was produced within the TRW propulsion-analysis/reporting lineage serving MSC, with `NAS9-8166` appearing on the adjacent NTRS records.

This materially improves search strategy for the missing Apollo 13 supplement. High-value catalog terms now include:

- exact title: `Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation`;
- Apollo 13 / LM-7;
- TRW Systems Group;
- contract `NAS9-8166`;
- Seto / Barrows / propulsion systems personnel as possible bibliographic cross-reference terms;
- MSC parent report `MSC-02680`;
- publication month October 1970.

## Evidence boundary

This pattern does **not** prove any of the following for Apollo 13 Supplement 2:

- that its report number was `MSC-02680-SUPPL-2`;
- that its TRW number shared the `11176-H...` sequence;
- that Seto, Barrows, Avvenire, or Wood authored it;
- that the October 1970 version preserved the same report structure or data products as Apollo 10/12/14;
- that adjacent-mission numerical thrust, mixture-ratio, mass-flow, or specific-impulse values apply to LM-7.

`MSC-02680-SUPPL-2` remains only a structurally plausible archival search key until a primary catalog record, scan, cover page, citation, or distribution record verifies it.

## Search result boundary

Exact-title, Apollo-13, and `MSC-02680-SUPPL-2` web/NTRS searches in this pass did not expose a direct public catalog record or scan for the missing report. That negative retrieval result is not evidence that the document is lost.

## Model consequence

No new LM-7 propulsion constants are authorized by this research pass. The current PC+2 numerical model must continue to distinguish documented Apollo 13 commanded profile / blowdown behavior from unrecovered LM-7 calibrated thrust, mass flow, mixture ratio, and effective specific impulse.
