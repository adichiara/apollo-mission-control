# Research note 232 — NAS9-8634 public digital retrieval boundary

Date: 2026-09-17
Status: **primary archival scope statement retained; no digitized contract file recovered**

## Question

Can the next unresolved Kollsman `NAS9-8634` LMS scope question be resolved from publicly searchable NASA primary-source systems before requesting the RG 255 E.155B1 files?

## Primary-source search result

Targeted public searches were run for exact and variant forms of `NAS9-8634` / `NAS 9-8634` together with Kollsman, Lunar Module Simulator, LMS, MSC, and KSC. No digitized NASA contract, statement of work, acceptance procedure, or technical report was recovered that exposes the subsystem-level scope of `NAS9-8634`.

The controlling primary locator therefore remains the NASA/NARA RG 255 preliminary inventory, series **E.155B1 — Project Files on the Lunar Module Simulator, 1968–1970**. Its description explicitly says the series contains correspondence, memoranda, statement of work, program and procurement plans, acceptance test plans and procedures, progress and technical reports, operations manuals, photographs of surface models, and copies of drawings; most correspondence is with Kollsman Instrument Corporation and concerns administration of `NAS9-8634` for construction of LM simulators at MSC and KSC. The inventory gives old accession `72A794` and location `A-23-16-6`.

Primary locator: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARA-SW/Rg255-1.pdf (RG 255 inventory p. 25).

## What this establishes

The unresolved scope question has reached a **file-level archival retrieval boundary**. Public indexing currently adds no defensible subsystem assignment beyond the E.155B1 series description.

This is useful negative retrieval evidence: repeated generic web/NTRS searching for the contract number should not be treated as a substitute for obtaining the E.155B1 statement of work, procurement/acceptance records, or related correspondence.

## Evidence boundary

The failed public retrieval does **not** establish that:

- `NAS9-8634` excluded or included MEP, EVDE, AOT, or any other named subsystem;
- Kollsman replaced Farrand;
- the contract configuration was unchanged through Apollo 13;
- any simulator output was routed to a Mission Control station;
- no digitized copy exists outside the search surfaces attempted.

## Next action

Prioritize direct retrieval of **E.155B1 / old accession `72A794` / location `A-23-16-6`**, requesting first the statement of work, procurement plan, acceptance plans/procedures, drawings, surface-model photographs, and Kollsman correspondence that identify contract scope and dated configuration. Continue E.155B MEP/EVDE retrieval in parallel. Build the vendor/configuration matrix only after file-level text is recovered.
