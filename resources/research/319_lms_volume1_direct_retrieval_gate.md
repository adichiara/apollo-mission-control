Date: 2026-09-18

## Question

Can the public `lms_instructors_handbook_vol1.pdf` scan now be promoted to the recovered copy of `LMA-790-2-LMS`, Volume I / MSC accession `67-14186`?

## Primary-source-first check

NASA/KSC `GP-642` provides the strongest directly searchable primary bibliographic control found in this pass. Entry 346 identifies:

- Grumman Aircraft Engineering Corporation;
- **Lunar Module Mission Simulator Instructors Handbook**;
- **Volume 1 — Simulator Description**;
- Volume II Section 1 — Simulator Operation;
- Volume II Section 4 — Instructor Activity for Interface Operation;
- **1 April 1967**;
- **GAEC LMA 790-2-LMS, Vols. I and II**.

Primary source: NASA/KSC `GP-642`, *Scientific and Technical Reports Relating to Project Apollo*, 28 February 1969, entry 346. NTRS PDF: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/19700025401/downloads/19700025401.pdf

The public Virtual AGC/ibiblio document directory independently exposes a file named `lms_instructors_handbook_vol1.pdf` (150 MB). That filename and the primary bibliography's title/volume description are strongly compatible, but the directory listing does not expose the scan's title, document-control, revision, or effectivity pages.

Public candidate: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/Documents/lms_instructors_handbook_vol1.pdf

## Retrieval result

The candidate cannot yet be promoted to a page-verified archival identity. In this research environment the 150 MB PDF exceeds the web fetcher's direct-document size limit, and no separately indexed NTRS record for `67-14186` / `LMA-790-2-LMS` was recovered. Therefore the required title/control-page comparison could not be completed in this pass.

This is a retrieval limitation, not evidence that the public scan is a different document.

## Evidence boundary

The following are now safe:

1. NASA itself bibliographically identifies `LMA 790-2-LMS` Volume I as **Simulator Description** and dates the handbook family 1 April 1967 in `GP-642`.
2. A public 150 MB scan explicitly labeled as LMS instructor handbook Volume 1 exists in the Virtual AGC document collection.
3. Identity of that scan with MSC accession `67-14186` remains unverified until its internal document-control pages are read.

Do **not** infer from filename/title compatibility alone:

- revision or issue status;
- site or mission effectivity;
- DDP-224 count or workload allocation;
- program-loading/common-memory architecture;
- Apollo 13 configuration;
- numerical cadence, tolerances, or executable model constants.

## Next retrieval action

Obtain the first document-control pages of the public scan by a tool/path capable of handling the 150 MB object, then compare exact report number, title, issue/revision markings, and dates against `67-14186`, `NAS9-1100`, and the conflicting NASA/MSC 1 April / 15 May 1967 issue-date records. Only after that comparison should technical page extraction begin.
