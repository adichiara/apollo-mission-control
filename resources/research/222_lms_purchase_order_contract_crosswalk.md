# Research note 222 — LMS purchase-order / NASA contract crosswalk

Date: 2026-09-16  
Status: **CONTRACT CROSSWALK RECOVERED; acceptance-procedure technical contents still unresolved.**

## Question

Can the `P.O. 2-24481C` statement reported on the title page of acceptance procedure `1L5-102-(H)` be independently tied to the Apollo LM Mission Simulator program and NASA contract `NAS9-1100`, without treating the auction catalog as technical evidence?

## Primary-source-first result

A 1973 Singer/Link corporate technical publication, *Link Training Systems by Singer*, contains a program table for Link simulation systems. The table identifies:

- `P.O. 2-24481C (NASA-1100)`;
- customer: `Grumman A/C Corp. (NASA)`;
- application: `LEM`.

Digitized scan:
https://www.bitsavers.org/pdf/singer/link/Link_Training_Systems_by_Singer_197311.pdf

This is direct contractor-family evidence from Link's successor corporate publication and independently connects the purchase-order number reported for `1L5-102-(H)` to the Grumman/NASA LEM simulator program.

Separately, the NASA/NARA corporate index identifies Grumman LMS documentation under contract `NAS9-1100`, including:

- `LMA-790-2-LMS`, Volume II Section 7, *Simulator Output Tables*, 1 Jul 1967, accession `*67-16127`;
- other `LMA-790-2-LMS` handbook sections dated 1 Apr 1967.

Primary NASA/NARA index scan:
https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARASWoverflow/CorporateIndex.pdf

## What is now established

- `P.O. 2-24481C` is independently associated with Grumman/NASA's LEM simulator work in a Link/Singer program record.
- The Link/Singer record renders the associated NASA number as `NASA-1100`; NASA/NARA's LMS document index renders the formal Grumman contract as `NAS9-1100`.
- The purchase-order lineage and the `LMA-790-2-LMS` handbook family therefore converge on the same Grumman/NASA LM Mission Simulator program context.
- This strengthens the document-identity provenance of the `1L5-102-(H)` retrieval lead beyond the auction description alone.

## Evidence boundary

Do **not** infer:

- that `NASA-1100` in the Singer table is a formally exact transcription of `NAS9-1100` rather than the publication's abbreviated rendering;
- the contents of Section E, Paragraph 5 of P.O. 2-24481C;
- what Modification Kit No. 1 changed;
- any acceptance test observable, stimulus, tolerance, pass/fail criterion, result, model revision, or Apollo 13 effectivity;
- that the 1967 acceptance procedure applies unchanged to the 1970 mission-era LMS configuration.

The technical acceptance boundary from research note 221 remains intact.

## Project consequence

The acceptance-procedure retrieval track is now better keyed for archival/catalog searching: search on all of `1L5-102-(H)`, `2-24481C`, Link/General Precision, Grumman, and `NAS9-1100`/`NASA-1100`.

Direct recovery of the manual or RG 255 E.155B1 acceptance/correlation records remains necessary before any numerical or station-visible validation claim can advance.
