# 140 — Apollo 13 DPS Supplement 2 publication and retrieval boundary

## Question

Was Apollo 13 Mission Report Supplement 2, *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation*, actually published, or did it remain only a planned/in-preparation report?

## Primary-source chain

The September 1970 *Apollo 13 Mission Report* lists Supplement 2 with status **Preparation**.

Later official NASA mission reports update the same Apollo 13 supplement table:

- *Apollo 14 Mission Report* (May 1971): Apollo 13 Supplement 2 — *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation* — **October 1970**.
- *Apollo 15 Mission Report* (1971): same title — **October 1970**.
- *Apollo 16 Mission Report* (August 1972): same title — **October 1970**.
- *Apollo 17 Mission Report* likewise carries the October 1970 date.

This repeated official retrospective listing is sufficient to establish that the supplement moved beyond the September 1970 preparation state and was recorded by NASA as an October 1970 publication.

Sources:

- Apollo 13 Mission Report, September 1970, NTRS 19710003598.
- Apollo 14 Mission Report, May 1971: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/afj/ap14fj/pdf/a14_mission-report.pdf
- Apollo 15 Mission Report, Appendix E: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/alsj/a15/a15mrp9.pdf
- Apollo 16 Mission Report, NTRS 19720026127.
- Apollo 17 Mission Report, Appendix E (cross-check).

## DOCUMENTED

- Apollo 13 Supplement 2 existed as a named Mission Report supplement.
- Its title was *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation*.
- NASA's later official mission-report tables record its publication date as **October 1970**.

## PUBLIC RETRIEVAL STATUS

A public copy was **not located in this research pass**.

The Apollo 13 document index at the Apollo Flight Journal currently exposes the main Mission Report, Supplement 1, and many other postflight documents, but does not list Supplement 2.

The Virtual AGC mission-report library likewise lists Apollo 13 Supplement 1 but not Supplement 2, despite listing descent-propulsion final-flight evaluations for several adjacent missions.

Searches of currently indexed NTRS results using the exact title and Apollo 13 mission terms returned the main mission report and later tables referencing Supplement 2, but not a direct Supplement 2 record.

Absence from these public indexes is **not evidence that the report is lost**. It only establishes that the report/identifier was not recovered here.

## Model consequence

The project's highest-value LM-7 propulsion source should now be described as:

> a **published but currently unrecovered** Apollo 13 Mission Report Supplement 2, *Descent Propulsion System Final Flight Evaluation* (October 1970).

This is stronger than treating the source as merely planned.

Do not substitute Apollo 10/12/14/15 DPS final-flight numerical values for the missing Apollo 13 data. Adjacent-mission reports may be used to learn the evaluation method, likely data products, terminology, and report structure.

## Adjacent-report identifier pattern

NTRS records for adjacent DPS final-flight evaluations show a consistent parent-report/supplement identifier convention:

- Apollo 12 DPS Supplement 5: `MSC-01855-SUPPL-5`, with TRW report `TRW-11176-H585-R0-00-SUPPL-5`;
- Apollo 14 DPS Supplement 5: `MSC-04112-SUPPL-5`, with TRW report `TRW-17618-H219-R0-00-SUPPL-5`;
- Apollo 15 DPS Supplement 4: `MSC-05161-SUPPL-4`, with TRW report `TRW-20029-H084-R0-00-SUPPL-4`.

Because the Apollo 13 parent Mission Report is `MSC-02680` and the missing document is Supplement 2, **`MSC-02680-SUPPL-2` is a structurally plausible search key**.

It is **not** recorded as the Apollo 13 supplement's verified report number unless a primary catalog record, scan, or bibliographic citation confirms it. Likewise, no TRW report number is inferred.
