# Research note 331 — R-567 Section 5 Revision 4 change-scope boundary

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: **Revision-4 change inventory materially narrowed; Apollo 11 equation effectivity still open**

## Question

Does the surviving R-567 Section 5 control history support the assumption that Section 5 Revision 4 itself incorporated the Apollo 11 landing changes PCR-670 and PCR-700?

## Primary evidence

The MIT/IL R-567 Section 5 Revision 8 artifact preserves an earlier-change inventory under the explicit heading **Incorporated in Rev. 4 of GSOP**. The recovered list runs from PCR 254.2 through PCN 755 and includes PCR 636, *November, 1968 GSOP Section 5 Corrections in Landing Program*. It does **not** list PCR-670 or PCR-700 in the recovered Revision-4 inventory.

The separately controlled June 1969 R-567 Section 2 Revision 4 cover is explicitly for **LUMINARY 1A (Rev. 099)** and does list PCR-670 (*Simplification of Landing Programs*) and PCR-700 (*Improve the Rate of Descent Mode (P66) Performance*).

These are section-specific revision histories. Equal revision numbers across R-567 sections cannot therefore be treated as a synchronized program-wide release state. In particular, the fact that Section 2 Revision 4 is explicitly LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099 does not prove that Section 5 Revision 4 contains PCR-670/PCR-700 or is the final Apollo 11-effective Section 5 state.

The later Revision 11 cumulative sheet remains useful but carries Revision-7 dagger annotations. The Revision 8 artifact independently exposes the earlier-change grouping; the two artifacts should be reconciled rather than flattened into a single inferred chronology.

## Consequence

This changes the retrieval strategy. **Section 5 Revision 4 remains a historical anchor, but it is no longer safe to treat it as the presumed Apollo 11 final equation target.** The next controlled task is to identify the Section 5 revision/change state in which PCR-670 and/or PCR-700 entered the Section 5 documentation, then tie that state to LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099. The missing `69-FS-3` remains the preferred parallel route to Apollo 11 programmed guidance equations.

No later Section 5 equations are promoted backward, and no executable or station-facing behavior changes.

## Evidence status

- **DOCUMENTED:** the surviving Section 5 Revision 8 artifact groups PCR 636 and the recovered 254.2–755 inventory under “Incorporated in Rev. 4 of GSOP.”
- **DOCUMENTED:** PCR-670 and PCR-700 are absent from that recovered Section 5 Revision-4 inventory.
- **DOCUMENTED:** Section 2 Revision 4, June 1969, explicitly names LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099 and lists PCR-670/PCR-700.
- **SUPPORTED INTERPRETATION:** R-567 section revision numbers are section-specific and cannot be assumed to represent synchronized content/effectivity across sections.
- **UNRESOLVED:** which Section 5 revision/change state first documented PCR-670/PCR-700; the exact Apollo 11-effective P63–P66 pages/equations/cadence; and `69-FS-3` retrieval.
