# Research note 330 — Apollo 11 R-567 Section 5 Revision 4 control-index recovery

Date: 2026-09-19
Status: **historical Section 5 Revision 4 control state recovered indirectly; Apollo 11 technical pages still unrecovered**

## Question

Can the missing LUMINARY 1A-effective R-567 Section 5 target be narrowed using a surviving primary R-567 artifact without projecting later LUMINARY equations backward?

## Primary evidence

The surviving MIT/IL `R-567` Section 5, Revision 11, *Guidance Equations* scan preserves earlier revision-index sheets in its front matter. One embedded sheet is explicitly headed **Section No. 5 — Guidance Equations (Revision 4)**. Its visible first sheet lists PCR 636 (*November, 1968 GSOP Section 5 Corrections in Landing Program*) and other change identifiers.

A critical control detail on that same sheet is now explicit: the dagger legend says **additional material added in Revision 7**. PCR 636 is not dagger-marked, while several entries on the preserved Revision 4 sheet are. Therefore the sheet is a later cumulative historical index, not a pristine snapshot in which every visible row can automatically be attributed to Revision 4. PCR 636 is supported as Revision-4 material by the sheet/legend; dagger-marked rows must not be used as Revision-4 evidence merely because they appear under the Revision-4 heading.

This is primary-document evidence that an R-567 **Section 5 Revision 4** control state existed and provides a concrete revision-index recovery route from the later controlled GSOP artifact. It is not permission to use Revision 11 equations for Apollo 11.

The independently recovered June 1969 R-567 Section 2 Revision 4 control sheet is explicitly for **LUMINARY 1A (Rev. 099)** and lists PCR-670 (*Simplification of Landing Programs*) and PCR-700 (*Improve the Rate of Descent Mode (P66) Performance*). Together, these records make Section 5 Revision 4 the strongest currently identified historical revision target, but they do **not yet prove** that every Section 5 Revision 4 page in the later cumulative artifact was the exact Apollo 11 flight-effective page set.

## Boundary

Do not infer from the embedded historical index alone:

- that Section 5 Revision 4 was the final Apollo 11 flight-effective Section 5 state;
- that dagger-marked later additions belonged to Revision 4;
- that every later cumulative scan page carrying Revision 4 ancestry is unchanged from the Apollo 11 period;
- exact P63-P66 equations or cadence;
- that PCR-670 or PCR-700 affected a particular Section 5 page unless the relevant revision-index sheet/page marker establishes it;
- any controller-facing display or station product.

Later Revision 8/11 equation text remains comparison evidence only unless an unchanged-page/effectivity chain is demonstrated.

## Consequence

The next retrieval step is narrower than a generic search for “LUMINARY 1A Section 5”: recover/inspect the complete **Section 5 Revision 4 revision-index pair and its P63-P66 affected pages**, preserving the later-addition legend when interpreting the cumulative index, then establish whether the relevant page states can be tied to LUMINARY 1A Rev. 099. `69-FS-3` remains the preferred parallel complete-equation source.

No executable or station-facing change is justified by this note.
