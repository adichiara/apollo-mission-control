## Catalog consequence

The air-ground source now serves two controlled purposes: (1) crew/CAPCOM wording and (2) radio-side timing anchors. It still cannot establish Garman→Bales→Flight wording, internal latency, overlap, named circuit, or keyset privileges.

The restored GUIDO L/R and NASA `792-AAI` sources should be aligned against these anchors before cross-loop chronology is promoted to documented status.
