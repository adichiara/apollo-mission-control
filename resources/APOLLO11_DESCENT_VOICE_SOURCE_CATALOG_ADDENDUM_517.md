## Catalog consequence

The project's voice evidence must remain partitioned. Air-ground wording can be supported from `AS11_TEC`; internal controller wording and timing require the primary GUIDO L/R / `792-AAI` recordings or other Mission-G internal-loop records. Do not reverse-engineer Garman/Bales/Flight dialogue from CAPCOM's subsequent crew transmission.
