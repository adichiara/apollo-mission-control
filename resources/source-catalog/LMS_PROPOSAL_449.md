## Evidence use

Use for proposed LMS mathematical-model vocabulary, flow architecture, variable relationships, and as a retrieval/crosswalk aid for later LMS model documentation.

## Boundary

The document is explicitly a proposal artifact. Its restrictive-data legend states that it was furnished in response to the RFP for proposal evaluation and separately addresses rights following a contract award. Therefore do **not** treat proposed equations, constants, interfaces, timing, computer allocation, or configuration as accepted/delivered LMS behavior without later corroboration.

For historical-profile use, cross-check candidate facts against later sources such as the 1965 LMS math-model reports, `LMA-790-2-LMS` documentation, acceptance/configuration records, and Apollo 13-period evidence.
