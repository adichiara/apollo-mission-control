## Evidence use

This catalog controls retrieval identity only. The index does not establish DDP-224 count/allocation, program ownership, common-memory organization, visual interfaces, site effectivity, or Apollo 13 configuration.

Existing project records contain an Apr. 1 versus May 15, 1967 bibliographic-date discrepancy. Preserve it until title/revision pages or records-management evidence explain the difference.
