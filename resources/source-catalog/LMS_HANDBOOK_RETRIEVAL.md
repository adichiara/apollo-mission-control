## `LMA-790-2-LMS` — NASA bibliographic control

Primary NASA/MSC corporate-author control identifies two adjacent Grumman handbook records under contract `NAS9-1100`, both dated 15 May 1967:

| MSC accession | Report | Identified content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `67-14186` | `LMA-790-2-LMS` | *Lunar Module Mission Simulator Instructors Handbook. Volume I — Simulator Description* |
| `67-14187` | `LMA-790-2-LMS` | *Lunar Module Mission Simulator Instructors Handbook. Vol. II — Simulator Operation Sections 1 and 4* |

Source: NASA/MSC *Corporate Author Index*, Grumman Aircraft Engineering Corp. entries: https://www.ibiblio.org/apollo/NARASWoverflow/CorporateIndex.pdf

NASA `GP-642`, an independently compiled NASA scientific-and-technical reports bibliography, also catalogs the LMS handbook family under `NAS9-1100` and gives **1 April 1967**. This independently supports the earlier April date already present in NASA indexing but does not explain the difference from the corporate index's 15 May date.

## Evidence use

This catalog controls retrieval identity only. Bibliographic records do not establish DDP-224 count/allocation, program ownership, common-memory organization, visual interfaces, site effectivity, or Apollo 13 configuration.

The Apr. 1 versus May 15, 1967 discrepancy remains open. Preserve both dates until title/revision pages or records-management evidence explain whether they represent publication, receipt, cataloging, revision, or another event.

## Retrieval order

1. `67-14186` — Volume I simulator description; inspect title/revision/effectivity pages first.
2. `67-14187` — Volume II Sections 1 and 4.
3. `67-16127` and other separately indexed sections only after their identities are independently verified.

For archive requests include `LMA-790-2-LMS`, `NAS9-1100`, both accessions, and both 1 Apr. and 15 May 1967 dates to avoid false negatives caused by catalog-date mismatch.
