# PC+2 Backroom / Staff Support Room Sources

Status: active source supplement for the first-playable backroom/support-room scope boundary.

## Primary sources

### Apollo 13 Review Board appendix — Mission Control Center activities

NASA, *Report of Apollo 13 Review Board*, Appendix A, Part A4, Mission Control Center Activities.

Relevant findings:
- MOCR operations were supported by Staff Support Rooms located adjacent to the control room.
- The report identifies Flight Dynamics, Flight Director, Vehicle Systems, and other support-room functions.
- Flight Dynamics SSR provided detailed trajectory/guidance analysis and external technical interfaces for the MOCR flight-dynamics team.
- Vehicle Systems SSR monitored detailed vehicle-system status and trends and supported malfunction detection, isolation, correction, and circumvention.

Implementation use: establishes that Apollo 13 was not a front-room-only operation and constrains any simulator simplification that omits or abstracts support-room work.

### Flight Control Division Mission Operations Report — Apollo 13, 28 April 1970

NASA Manned Spacecraft Center, *Mission Operations Report: Apollo 13*.

Relevant finding: General Comments and Recommendations explicitly commend EECOM SSR personnel, especially EPS specialists, for support during the oxygen-tank anomaly and entry power-management planning.

Implementation use: mission-specific confirmation that SSR personnel could materially contribute to real-time technical analysis and troubleshooting.

### Apollo 13 Press Kit

NASA, *Apollo 13 Press Kit*, 1970.

Relevant finding: each MOCR operations group had a same-floor staff support room for detailed monitoring and analysis; additional supporting MCC areas included meteorology, the space-environment console, SPAN, recovery operations, and ALSEP support.

Implementation use: corroborates the Apollo 13-era support-room architecture and prevents the first playable's seven/five-seat player model from being described as a complete historical staffing reconstruction.

## First-playable rule

Backroom/SSR functions are **omitted as playable roles for now**, not declared historically absent. Do not create generic automated expert advice or silently grant front-room players unsupported analysis. If a scenario decision materially depends on a support-room product, research and model that dependency explicitly before implementation.
