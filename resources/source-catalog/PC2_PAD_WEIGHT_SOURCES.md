# Apollo 13 PC+2 maneuver-pad weight sources

## NASA — Apollo 13 Technical Air-to-Ground Voice Transcription

- NTRS record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20160014370
- Publication date: 1970-04-01
- NTRS document ID: `20160014370`
- NASA indexed transcript PDF: https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/as13-tec.pdf
- Source class: primary / contemporaneous mission transcription
- Relevant passage: transcript page 329, GET 077:55:24 through 077:59:50

### Supports

- final PC+2 P30 LM maneuver PAD TIG `079:27:38.30`;
- CSM weight `62480 lb`;
- LM weight `33452 lb`;
- crew readback of both weights;
- CAPCOM confirmation of the readback;
- association of these weights with the final operational maneuver PAD.

### Does not support

- exact RTCC mass-accounting convention;
- exact physical ignition mass;
- whether ullage or DPS startup propellant is included/excluded at a particular mass epoch;
- internal RTCC/Flight Dynamics computation fields.
