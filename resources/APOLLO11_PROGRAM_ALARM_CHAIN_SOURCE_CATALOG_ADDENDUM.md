## Targeted recovery pass

A source-recovery pass specifically targeted: (1) a post-final-simulation Apollo 11 mission-rule change page/change notice, (2) the Bales/Garman alarm cue sheet or an authenticated contemporary copy, and (3) revised Apollo-11-effective Guidance/training procedures from the final-simulation-to-landing interval. No contemporary artifact in those classes was recovered from the accessible sources.

Secondary/retrospective accounts contain recurrence-language variants such as `if it doesn't recur` or `if it doesn't come up too much`. Those accounts are useful leads but are not promoted into the source catalog as the missing operational rule, because the exact contemporary sheet/procedure remains absent.

## Catalog consequence

The operational alarm chain can be represented at role level: computer-specialist support informs GUIDANCE; GUIDANCE recommends; FLIGHT disposes; CAPCOM relays. Primary postflight engineering documents support alarm mechanics, but the available April/May mission-rule copies cannot be cited as the late-preflight alarm rule because their dates precede the simulation-driven review.

Under D-024, the exact written criterion/cue-sheet wording is now **BLOCKED on named source recovery**. Reopen only if a post-simulation change page/change notice, authenticated cue sheet, or dated revised Guidance/training procedure becomes accessible. Until then, do not encode a numeric recurrence threshold or universal `1201/1202 = GO` rule.

## Evidence status

- **DOCUMENTED / PRIMARY:** crew-facing alarm reports and GO calls; front-room GUIDANCE→FLIGHT recommendation/disposition.
- **DOCUMENTED / PRIMARY POSTFLIGHT ENGINEERING:** alarm occurrence/mechanism and restart behavior.
- **DOCUMENTED / NASA RETROSPECTIVE:** Garman support relationship and preparation of an alarm list.
- **BLOCKED / NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY:** exact late-preflight written decision criterion and cue-sheet wording.
- **UNRESOLVED BUT NOT INFERRED:** exact support-room words/loop topology and exact Mission-G alarm display basis.
