## Catalog consequence

The operational alarm chain can now be represented at role level: computer-specialist support informs GUIDANCE; GUIDANCE recommends; FLIGHT disposes; CAPCOM relays. The simulator must not make the alarm number alone determinative until the contemporary GO/abort criterion is recovered.

## Evidence status

- **DOCUMENTED / PRIMARY:** crew-facing alarm reports and GO calls; front-room GUIDANCE→FLIGHT recommendation/disposition.
- **DOCUMENTED / NASA RETROSPECTIVE:** Garman support relationship and overload interpretation.
- **UNRESOLVED:** contemporary written decision criterion, exact support-room words/loop topology, exact Mission-G alarm display basis.
