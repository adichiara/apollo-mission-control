## Catalog consequence

The project now has a Mission-G archival recorder map and an accessible restored primary-audio corpus exposing GUIDO left/right. This is sufficient to move the next alarm-traffic question from source discovery to direct recording inspection. Searchable automated transcripts may locate candidate timestamps, but any historical claim about participants, wording, or sequence must be verified against the restored audio.

The audio does **not** collapse the configuration boundary. Hearing a voice on a GUIDO recording cannot by itself identify the named loop carrying it or establish talk privilege. The exact Bales/Garman loop remains unresolved. PHO-TN401 and a Mission-G-effective station/keyset record remain document-recovery targets.

The defensible alarm chain remains **guidance-software back-room support → GUIDO/Bales → FLIGHT/ground decision chain → CAPCOM → crew**.
