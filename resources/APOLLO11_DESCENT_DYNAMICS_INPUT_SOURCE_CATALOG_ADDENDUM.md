## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** NASA ALSJ now supplies a genuine LM-5-era **Volume-I Subsystems Data excerpt** with 15 December 1968 basic date and explicit LM-5 versus LM-6-and-subsequent configuration language. This materially improves Volume-I provenance but does **not** recover the DPS section, so exact LM-5 FTP calibration, delivered thrust/Isp, and PDI mass remain unresolved. The official BET Volume II and Apollo 11 DPS Supplement 7 remain **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**.
