## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** Apollo 11 now has a contemporary mission-specific preflight DPS thrust specification distinct from the later generic design requirement. This narrows the model provenance boundary but does not close delivered thrust or Isp. Mission-specific postflight LM masses remain documented only at named events, with exact PDI mass unresolved. The official BET Volume II and Apollo 11 DPS Supplement 7 remain **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**, although Supplement 7's September 1970 publication is independently documented.
