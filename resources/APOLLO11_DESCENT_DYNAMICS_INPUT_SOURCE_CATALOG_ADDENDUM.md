## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** The handbook lineage now bounds the mission-applicable Volume-I target to **LMA790-3-LM 5 and Subsequent dated 15 June 1969**. The 92.5-percent automatic-throttle ceiling is supported by pre-Apollo-11 handbook/control documentation. The missing discriminating evidence remains the **LM-5 DPS performance/design pages themselves (or later-carried pages with 15 June-or-earlier effective dates)**: exact LM-5 FTP force calibration, delivered thrust/Isp, and PDI mass remain unresolved. The official BET Volume II and Apollo 11 DPS Supplement 7 remain **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**.
