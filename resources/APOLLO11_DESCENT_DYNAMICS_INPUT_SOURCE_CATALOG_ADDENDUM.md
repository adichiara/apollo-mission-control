## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** Purdue's official MSA 5 finding aid/catalog now identifies three contemporaneous Apollo 11 planning items directly bearing on FTP/throttle-down calibration and sensitivity. The two 8 July records add explicit archive identifiers and show that engine dispersion, LM weight, and PDI dispersions were analyzed as throttle-down-time variables. Their contents remain **BLOCKED ON ARCHIVAL ITEM RECOVERY**; no numerical force, mass, or dispersion is inferred from titles. Page 2.3-25 remains blocked on legible primary-page recovery. Supplement 7 identifiers/content remain blocked on bibliographic/archival recovery. Exact LM-5 FTP force calibration, delivered thrust/Isp, and PDI mass remain unresolved; BET Volume II remains **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**.
