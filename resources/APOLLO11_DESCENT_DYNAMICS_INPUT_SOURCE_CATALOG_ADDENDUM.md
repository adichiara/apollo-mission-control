## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** Page-level recovery now proves the **92.5-percent throttle hard stop was present in the 15 June 1969 LM-5 handbook state**. The missing discriminating evidence is narrower: DPS §2.3 performance/design pages with LM-5-effective dates, LM-5 acceptance/postflight performance, exact PDI mass, and Supplement 7. Exact LM-5 FTP force calibration and delivered thrust/Isp remain unresolved; BET Volume II and Supplement 7 remain **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**.
