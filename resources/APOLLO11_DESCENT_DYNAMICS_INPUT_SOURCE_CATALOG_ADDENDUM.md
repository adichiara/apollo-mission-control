## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** Mission-specific postflight LM masses are primary-source documented at named events, but exact PDI mass remains unresolved. NASA SP-4029 and NASA 20080013635 now provide two distinct examples of later event/value reuse that cannot override the primary mission record. The official BET Volume II and Apollo 11 DPS Supplement 7 remain **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**. Exact delivered thrust/Isp remain unresolved.
