## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** A public primary LM-5-and-subsequent Volume-II AOH is now recovered, establishing configuration-specific operational-procedure provenance. It does not provide the missing FTP force calibration. Apollo 11 primary sources establish the 9,870-lbf mission-facing preflight specification and LM-5 use of FTP; later subsystem documentation establishes mature-design 92.5-percent FTP / 9,870-lbf nominal fixed-full-throttle semantics but remains configuration-qualified. Exact LM-5 FTP calibration, delivered thrust/Isp, and PDI mass remain unresolved. The official BET Volume II and Apollo 11 DPS Supplement 7 remain **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**.
