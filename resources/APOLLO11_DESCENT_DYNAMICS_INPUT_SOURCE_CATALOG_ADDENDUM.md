## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** The primary NASA-MSC transmittal proves that an **LM-5-specific Operational Calibration Curves Volume II dated 12 June 1969** existed in the Apollo 11 launch-data holdings and now provides the actionable retrieval key **FRC accession 72-A-1116 / RG 255 / agency box 71**. A neighboring Apollo 12 primary launch-data listing independently confirms the recurring three-volume vehicle-calibration series structure and identifies its **Vol. II as LM-6**, strengthening the interpretation of the Apollo 11 catalog entry without exposing LM-5 contents. The LM-5 volume remains **BLOCKED ON DOCUMENT RECOVERY**, so no DPS calibration value is claimed. The 5 June FTP study has a primary-source bibliographic crosswalk to **John P. Mayer / MSC MPAD / memorandum 69-FMZ2-149**, but its numerical result remains blocked. The June-base/July-addendum relationship for 69-FM-156 and the two 8 July records remain archival recovery targets. Page 2.3-25, Supplement 7 identifiers/content, exact LM-5 FTP force calibration, delivered thrust/Isp, PDI mass, and BET Volume II remain unresolved/blocked as documented.
