## Evidence status

**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** Page 2.3-25 remains **BLOCKED ON LEGIBLE PRIMARY PAGE-IMAGE / LEP RECOVERY**. Supplement 7 existence/title/date are documented, but its actual Apollo 11 report identifiers and contents remain **BLOCKED ON BIBLIOGRAPHIC / ARCHIVAL RECOVERY**. Apollo 10 primary evidence now establishes the packaging model: its MSC Supplement 7 wraps earlier TRW project report `11176-H314-R0-00`. Therefore the highest-value Apollo 11 search target is the underlying **LM-5 DPS Final Flight Evaluation project technical report**, not only the later MSC supplement wrapper. `MSC-00171-SUPPL-7` remains a search string, not sourced metadata. Exact LM-5 FTP force calibration, delivered thrust/Isp, and PDI mass remain unresolved; BET Volume II remains **BLOCKED ON NAMED SOURCE RECOVERY**.
