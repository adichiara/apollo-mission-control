**PARTIALLY DOCUMENTED.** The recurring operational-calibration series is now positively characterized by NASA/NTRS as telemetry/downlink/onboard-display measurement calibration. The LM-5 volume remains blocked on actual document recovery, so its channel coverage and values are unknown. The direct FTP memo and companion dispersion studies remain higher-priority numerical-recovery targets.
