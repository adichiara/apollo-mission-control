## Controlled boundary

The SC-012 pair is an early **AS-204A / Block I Command Module Apollo Mission Simulator** source family. It is potentially valuable for comparative simulator architecture, instructor-workbook organization, malfunction abstractions, and output concepts. It does **not** establish Apollo 13 LM LMS configuration, malfunction availability, LM equations, LMS scripting syntax, MCC routing, or 1970 instructor procedures.

Any technical claim imported from these manuals must therefore remain labeled **comparative/adjacent** until independently corroborated by LMS- and mission-effective evidence.
